In this district stands yet another repurposed building. What used to be a temple to [Ëlun](knowledge%20books/world%20religions/Gods/Ëlun.md) has now become a praying spot for all of the gods of the [New Material](knowledge%20books/is%20it%20a%20bird.%20No%20its%20a%20plane/New%20Material.md). [Krava­­ìr](Krava­­ìr)’s statue has two years ago been destroyed when one his [Ireclad](Ireclad) destroyed it in a fit of divine rage. Effort to rebuild has been done by Strider [Rakita](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Rakita.md) and after her passing [Frankie](Frankie). In a corner of the temple one may find the last remaining statue of [Ëlun](knowledge%20books/world%20religions/Gods/Ëlun.md), some people still come here to find solace in praying to a familiar deity despite their destruction.
