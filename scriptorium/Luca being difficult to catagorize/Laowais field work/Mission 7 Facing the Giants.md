The Mission seemed a simple checkup, yet due to the INCREDIBLY ‘necessary’ actions of Sir [Albedo](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Albedo.md) it had devolved into the uprising of a new social class. 

From our understanding there seemed to be a rival clan to our [hill giant](hill%20giant) puppet. [Ottask](Ottask) the chieftain has been at the head of his tribe for a while, put there as an ally due to the diligent efforts of Lady [Märchen](Märchen) despite the object of her ogrish husband. However another request for assistance had arrived, with [Ottask](Ottask) mentioning odd behaviour from a rivalling tribe. It is common for the half-a-dozen tribes in the north to skirmish against each other. However one started showing behaviour unalike their kin. We were sent in to investigate. 

  
  

It was in the middle of the Giant territory in the Noyau Ridge. Finding little interest, apart from the usual  on the way over we move to [Ottask](Ottask) and his problem with haste. Finding a use for their large goats as mounts in these treacherous mountains we make our way to the target. 

When we arrived we did not find any form of hostility. In fact our approach did not seem only to thick off the annoyed guard standing watch. Perhaps we were too small to be registered as a threat. We each found a desired way to enter this camp, from communication to stealth. A golden tip to whenever dealing with any of the [hill giant](hill%20giant) tribes. They are quite resistant to booze, but they sure do love drinking it. Bring plenty as a form of bribe.

  

We found out that this tribe was led by a more warlike, traditional, giant. One that did not seem to adhere to the new ‘mind over muscles’ way that [Ottask](Ottask) and his allies upheld. With a “might makes right” attitude he had been attacking other clans for their food storages as well as some prisoners. Using the scarce food to create a stable class hierarchy with him and his guards at the top. (While I deny the general intelligence of most of their kind, I do admit that this particular tribe seemed to have been able to evolve from the barbarist society, to a chiefdom and his ‘gefolgschaft’ but that is just me.) Leaving the hungry workers as a lower class to mine what appears to be unique resources that are traded off as another sign of advanced intellect in this savage society. 

  
  

Splitting into two groups we believed would yield us more results in our search. Why were these [giant](knowledge%20books/Taxonomical%20diffrences%20between%20diffrent%20humanoid%20races/giant.md)s doing these odd things and where did the mentioned hostility come from? While [Albedo](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Albedo.md) and [Oreo](Oreo) went out to the workers to find out. [Märchen](Märchen) and I stayed behind to discuss with the chief. The workers revealed that they were mining for scraps, that the boss kept their food to keep them weakened. But much more information they did not find out of them. Instead moving onto other plans of freeing them.

  

[Märchen](Märchen) and I therefore took a more peaceful approach. Discussing with the chief we found that not only does he enjoy booze, he loves himself. He believed his power and dominion were absolute and despised [Ottask](Ottask)’s way. Being able to use this to draw a glorious portrait we found out that a duel with his champion would let us be the victor and we could demand whatever answers we needed. Using my charm, I was able to make a deal with the tribe chief and managed to sabotage the fight in [Märchen](Märchen)’s favour by suggesting the champion to take it slowly. The champion in question appeared to have been a [vampire](vampire) giant, a very unique appearance. We could blame what the tribe called their accountant, [Märchen](Märchen) found out this small humanoid was a [vampire](vampire) that ensured that the mined resources were send elsewhere. Explaining their “advanced” societal structure.

  

On the day of the duel we saw [Märchen](Märchen) absolutely beat the snot out of the champion, clearly Haven’s strongest Goliath. While she may appear weak and frail as a wizard, I was able to convince the chieftain to make a little wager. My crate of booze if the champion won, against one of his guards if [Märchen](Märchen) won. Safe to say, I now have a giant butler. While the duel went on, [Albedo](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Albedo.md) and [Oreo](Oreo) were staging a revolution by riling the workers to go and attack the guards. Just as the champion fell to the ground, a pickaxe of one worker cleaved the chief’s head in twain. 

  

We never found the vampiric accountant, we never found what the resource mined was, never found why they were so hostile to begin with. But at least we solved the issue and delivered another tribe in our allied sphere of influence.

Solved it like true [Striders](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Striders.md).
