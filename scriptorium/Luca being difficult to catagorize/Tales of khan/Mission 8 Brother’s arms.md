report on the rightful heir mission written by [[Khan Bloodhand]] May 2026

(Transcribed by Guild Clerk [[Laowai Xhê]])

  
  

Send by some poem or whatnot we were told to investigate it. Apparently some watery tart was lobbying knives at anyone who accepted her challenge. I would never ignore a proper challenge but this one did not really peaked my interest. Yet Lady [[Frill]] and my brother seemed more than interested so I feared I had no choice but to follow them. [[Vivian]] accompanied us on our travels as well. 

  

Being one of the longest missions to the date, we had spent a total of 6 days away. With our start at the [[riftgate]] it was about the distance of 8 to 10 days away from [[Haven]]. What follows is a travel log taken by Lady [[Frill]] and indoctrinated in my map. 

  

We started moving South-East for 4 days. 

The first day was uneventful. Moving through the [[scarlands]] was nothing to write about. Some minor insectoids and imps that moved around us. They were smart enough not to dare and interfere with us. We couldn’t be bothered by them.

  

At the beginning of the second day we arrived at a new area at the time. The [[Verdan Scythe]], stupid name, should have called it the [[pinewood]] or something. Either way we moved further. We stumbled upon the coast of the [[Blue Expanse]] to our south. Huge cliffs made a pretty sight, with at the bottom of the bay a shipwreck which we took some time plundering further. Unbeknownst to us we were watched by an enemy.[[Vivian]] was the first to notice that we were surrounded by a familiar tribe of [[Goblins]]. The [[Gut Cutters]]. Yet to my surprise, I have fought them before next to [[Haven]], they have expanded all the way out here. Or perhaps this was where their stronghold sat. Whatever the case around the end of the second day we were ambushed by a party of [[goblins]]. Most of them were easily disposed of apart from one unique individual. A [[goblin]] lass wielding a hammer twice her size. She was a great adversary, being able to escape my grip not trough [[dexterity]] but through her own powerful display. She even managed to push me away. Yes I remember her fondly and if I ever got the chance, I’d be resurrecting her to serve my cause. ([[Khan]] proceeds to show me a gilded skull of presumably the goblin female he was fighting.) 

  
On the third day we continued our trip through the [pine forest](Verdan%20Scythe). We had received word that the [[Chromatic covenant]], an enemy [[draconid]] faction, had set their eyes upon us and that we should expect a challenge sooner or later. They seemed much more arrogant than we had originally planned, I had no interest in their mind games. The others seemed more troubled by the fact that the message was sent by a captured innocent man. At the end of the day we found a unique statue in the middle of the forest. A metallic constructed scorpion covered by runes. We believe this might have been a creation of the [[Gorro Ora Orcs]], an enemy [[orc]] faction.

  

On the fourth day we arrived at a clearing in the forest. We found several magically enchanted sheep that seemed to be afflicted by a multitude of spells. [[Frill]]’s clever insight allowed us to find out these were the [[Zutaagi Jigen orcs]] that seemed to have meddled once more with the wildmagic. As soon as we tried to interact and bring them to safety we were attacked by the previously mentioned [[Gorro Ora orcs]]. They were no match to us and I took my sweet time ‘interrogating’ one of our captives. We found out they have a strong chieftain set up, use runes they have learned from their superiors, and their camp being 2 to 3 days east from the current position.

  

Day 4 we continued further, passing flowerfields to our south. This day passed without much trouble. It is highly advised to explore these fields further but nothing more of note.

  

Day 5 we moved south, continuing through the [[Verdan Scythe]] with the flowerfields to our east. At the end of the day we were awoken by prisoners of the C[[hromatic Covenant]], saying that an attack will be launched by them during the night. Having the time to prepare we ensured that the area was covered in oil and ready to burn when they attacked. To much of my partners dismay, they used these prisoners as combatants. Quite sad but they had to be put down anyway, for their fear of the captors was misplaced for they should have feared me more. I put most out of their misery but it is clear Lady [[Frill]] was quite hit by them. This shows their capabilities and willingness to use underhanded tactics, yet also highlights the degree of arrogance and desire for combative challenges they have. 

  

Finally we arrived at our destination, a warm beach at the southern banks of the [[Blue Expanse]]. We did not see the challenger but she would soon have revealed her. Explaining matters of difficulty with the parums in her homeland or something, I was not really paying attention. She made use of moth spirits who turned to knights while dueling [[Baerrek]], and just like a true [[rockeater]] warrior [[Baerrek]] was able to defeat her and earn himself his new sword.
