Strider Report – Sheep and Big Birds
Party: [[Haru]] (me), [[Widdle]], [[Mirea]], [[Orchid]]
I met some new [[Striders]] today when we started our trip east to the goblin camp.
Their names are [[Widdle]], [[Mirea]], and [[Orchid]]. They all seem really cool. I still think
[[Vivian]] is cooler though.
We traveled for a few hours until we reached the [[goblin camp]]. There wasn’t much
left there anymore, only a broken building and some ruins. On the way, we met a
sheep-heard man who was looking for missing sheep. He said he lost around five
after counting them many times. He looked very worried, so we told him we would
help.
When we searched the camp, I saw something white and fluffy inside the broken
building. I thought it was a sheep, so I ran in right away. [[Mirea]] stopped me, and
then [[Widdle]] rushed in instead. It turned out it wasn’t a sheep at all, but a big
feather bird with a beak, a [[grifon]] or something like that.
I tried to climb it because I thought maybe I could ride it, I have some flying
experience with [[Vivian]] after all. It did not go well. I failed a few times, but I kept
trying. While we were dealing with it, a second bird arrived behind [[Orchid]]. We
defeated the first one. The second one landed, and I managed to jump on top of it. It
flew very high, but I lost my balance and it grabbed me with its claws and carried
me back to the broken building.
At first I thought it was dangerous, but it actually showed me its nest with baby
birds inside. They were pecking me, but I think they were just hungry. We
knocked the mother bird unconscious so it would stop attacking, and I fed the
babies while she was asleep. After that, we left them alone.
We kept searching and found sheep tracks leading south. We followed them for
about two hours and found three of the missing sheep grazing in a field. We
planned to surround them. [[Orchid]] used earth magic to help keep them from
running away, and together we managed to capture them safely.
After a short rest, we brought the sheep back to the sheep-heard. He was very
happy and thanked us many times for returning some of his sheep.
[[Mirea]] also talked to the sheep, which was very funny. She said some sheep like
tall grass and some like short grass. I don't know why I found that so funny.
They're just little fluffy creatures with very strong opinions about grass.
Animals are weird.
I like them.
