The rural area outside of [[Haven]] is dedicated to agriculture. The district is made up of small farms and homesteads, with fields as far as the eye can see stretching far and wide. A wide assortment of crops and pastures for livestock assures the survival of Haven’s residents. About a year ago, the district was ravaged by the [[Skarn Wyvern]] as it had decided to lair next to [[Haven]] and make the area around its hunting grounds. Only through sheer determination and the temporary loss of two more [[Striders]] was the beast finally felled allowing the district to slowly return to normal.

Now just about two months ago, the city but mostly the farms again fell prey to a hideous monster. This time it was a [[red dragon]] born from underneath [[the Verge]] that was unleashing its fury on the farmlands as the inner city of [[Haven]] had proven itself well defended. Only through the intervention of the [[Blue Dragon]] was the red one finally driven away.
