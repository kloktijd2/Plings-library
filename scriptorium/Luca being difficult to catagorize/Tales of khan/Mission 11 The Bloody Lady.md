Report on Operation Botanical written by [Khan Bloodhand](Khan%20Bloodhand) August 2026 (Transcribed by [Laowai Xhê](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Laowai%20Xhê.md))

  

It was a simple mission to find the [Crimson Lady](Crimson%20Lady), get her justification for transcribing on the pact, and to place a beacon to where we could launch later attacks should it be needed.  
  
In a mission far prior, my wife, my brother, and I had taken an operation to go and free an alleged [devil](devil) out of her shackles. (transcriber: Euh… .they did what!?) Some of her [demons](demons) had gotten the idea that my soul belongs to her and I wanted to ensure she knew it did not. We unleashed her and offered a lot of heads from her captors (the [gnolls](knowledge%20books/animals%20of%20the%20new%20material/gnolls.md)). I got my revenge and made a deal that she could have hell and we can have the overworld.

  

APPARENTLY, she has not been holding her end of the bargain. Been hearing stuff about bloodied sheeps popping up near the starter areas. Other [demons](demons) have been sighted too that sport a surprising amount of blood. I also heard crazy tales from [Albedo](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Albedo.md) that they saw me and [Märchen](Märchen) as blood statues with our abilities. I do highly dispute that, for if they truly had our abilities. They would not have made it out alive.

  

Now as we arrived into hell we fell into a thick fog area. Moving away from the sounds of the whirlpool, we go deeper into the fog. I have from this moment decided that this direction will be south. (tl: He can just do that?) As we went further south we were tormented from nightmares from the past. Unlike the [Chromatic Covenant](Chromatic%20Covenant) who kinda failed at getting the message across, this thing seems to be some automatically successful magicky stuff idk I didn’t bring my wife. 

  

So eventually after a day or so, it was hard to keep track. After all no day and night in hell, we arrived in a grassy plains. It was red tall grass as far as the eye could see but lacked any hills or trees. I therefore dubbed it the [NEVERPLAINS](NEVERPLAINS), and from now on everyone should follow this. (tl: Wow, a surprising show of creativity from the bearbrained oaf). 

  

We were attacked here by a horrible little collection of bugs. **TW Gore**: According to their design it seems that they should have been able to pass our heavy armored line. They would have burrowed into our skin with their sharp teeth and claws, and perhaps puppeteered us as walking armor. A fate we were luckily spared from through some clever combat from [EK](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/EK.md) and [Wollfrud](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Wollfrud.md). **TW done**

  

After resting up some more we continue our travel. We decided to move to the West, for we have travelled here before just from another part. That must mean to the east there is [the sea of lava](the%20sea%20of%20lava). Traveling some more [Vivian](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Vivian.md) seemed to have spot another threat but oh how wrong she was. This was not a threat for we found my 3 dear darling [demon spiders](demon%20spiders). These things are official parts of the [Rockeater](Rockeater) tribe and will be avenged should they ever be killed. Remember if you ever see them in the wilderness be sure to feed them live feed. They love [gnoll](gnoll) and have a good hunger for pigs. Luckily I brought a barrel of 1000 pounds of pork for the occasion ([pling note](pling%20note.md): how strong is this [Khan](Khan.md)) . Riding on their backs to the edge of her domain we witnessed the [blackstone obelisk](blackstone%20obelisk) that pierced the heavens and moved up to the overworld. 

  

She greeted us when we entered like old friends. At least I was treated kindly. We were offered food and drinks which were just a combination of bloodied things and pure blood. I even tasted my own blood in it. (tl: tf?) Either way it seems she has been pleased with my work on various occasions. I guess she and the bear do have some similarities in warrior shows of faith. I did drown the [Pyrehold bastion](Pyrehold%20bastion) in blood and cursed its ruins with the hanged and crucified presence of headless corpses. Whatever the case I explained the predicament and she said she never really agreed to it. She wasn’t a typical [demon](demon) that is bound by her word, pretty neat. She did not do it out of the love of scheming, she is not like that slimy [slime lord](slime%20lord). (Read Rudy’s reports). But when asked why she did not keep the other end of the bargain, that she could continue to send demons to me as some manner of training she said she believed me too busy. (Tl: soooo… Khan made a deal with a [demon lord](demon%20lord)… to keep him entertained by bloodshed. Why are we paying this guy a tier 4 wage again?)

  

Seeing that she was upfront with it all, and very willing to give us another warmup battle for some gold. I have decreed that the [Crimson Queen](Crimson%20Queen) is pretty alright. We will go there and give her a right scrap as was agreed. Let us hope for a clean battle. Who knows, maybe next time I can beat her and claim the title of [Demon Lord](Demon%20Lord) for myself. Well at least the warmup allowed me to beat a balor in single combat. (tl: excuse me what?)
