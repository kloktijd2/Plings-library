The Whistling Peaking
[[Zea]], [[Leona]], [[Rudy]][[, Albedo]]
We head northeast for 1.5 days before we reach The [[Silent Woods]]. After some character
conflict, we head further northeast for half a day before settling for the night.
During watch, [[Albedo]] sees a cloaked figure approach and wakes up the party. When it
approaches, it seems to be [[Henka]] but evil. [[Albedo]] attacks him, and when he is almost
defeated he turns into slime. A moment later, acid walls emerge around every party member
and enemies spawn. We defeat them.
We rest and continue northeast into The [[Silent Woods]]. We stumble upon a ruin where [[Zea]]
has been ambushed before. [[Albedo]] sneaks into the camp and sees dormant [[oozes]]. [[Zea]] and
[[Rudy]] start shooting fire at the camp while we leave for the flying island. We continue walking
northeast, and see a treeline in the distance. In the north we see a [[slime wall]] where the [[Red Wall]] should be. We hypothesize that the slime needs something to copy. We short rest.
In the second shift, [[Rudy]] gets woken up by the telepathic voice of whatever possesses
[[Henka]], and he says he is called [[the Informant]]. **public:**(A very powerful demon who was also
present in the Howling woods when the [[gnolls]] were performing their death ritual that [[Rudy]],
[[Oreo]], [[Laowai]] and [[Henka]] encountered. Knowing this, [[Rudy]] makes a deal with him for safe
passage. He destroys [[Rudy]]’s spellbook. To haunt [[Rudy]] of what happened, he shows us the
worst moments of that mission) **trusted striders only:**(Rudy makes a deal with something. He loses his spellbooks
and the other party gets shown memories of Rudy’s naughty behaviour on an acid wall. He
does this for more information.)
The acid walls display [[Rudy]], [[Oreo]], [[Henka]] and [[Laowai]]. They walk into the [[Howling Woods]],
and see a rune meaning “carnage” everywhere. They are pulsing with dark energy, but they
ignore it to wait nearby the [[Gnoll]] fortress, they wait while [[Wycian]] is not doing much. There is
a lot of magic coming out of the fortress, and only after more than 1 hour, they try to take
action. They destroy a tower and kill one of their leaders but get lost afterward. Then, they
see the destructive ritual start and wait, only [[Henka]] flees but gets killed by [[Gnolls]]. The
[[Howling Woods]] are destroyed with everything and everyone in it. **trusted striders only:** (Then, we see a battle for
[[Sheng Fu Keep]], where Laowai and Rudy obtain a piece of a magical crown. They never
report it and store it. We know where it is. [[Rudy]] is the crown’s bearer and protector. It is in
his possession. He is researching and gathering information to gather the other pieces to
restore the world. It is his mission and he can be trusted to do so.)
The memories end, and [[Leona]] and [[Albedo]] are pissed. Rudy tries to dissuade us from
putting this in the report. It won’t work.
We discuss this lengthily.
We go to sleep.
We wake up and see and go east towards the treeline. The forest is very lush, the area of
decay stops very abruptly. We assume that this is one of the four wonders, [[the Magical Forest]]. We head northeast towards the floating island. We happen upon a very well
camouflaged house among the trees. [[Nyx]] looks through an opening and sees 2 unmoving
trees in human form embracing each other in what seems like a dance. In the backs of the
trees, there are empty hand prints. [[Albedo]] puts his hand in and feels suffused with nature
energy. With [[Shamir]], it does not activate. [[Rudy]] investigates and says it is malevolent [[fey]]
energy. He removes his hand. Upon further investigation, we find a trapdoor and find that
everything is made of wood. We enter the trapdoor and see a wine cellar made of wood with
2 magical red roses growing out of a workbench. We pluck the flowers and give them to the
dancing trees, while we stand outside for safety. The petals from the flowers coalesce and
form into three large, red mothlike creature. They ask for professions of love, we give them
to each other. They show us a vision of what happened to the cottage. It shows 2 girls being
happy around this specific house, they grow into teenagers and profess their love with a
rose, and even later they have a beautiful wedding where they dance. Over time everything
turns into wood, they do while dancing together. The moths disappear. We leave a note
warning other [[Striders]].
When walking further towards the mountains, we discover an entire city being turned into
wood. We will explore the city further in another expedition. We call it [[Rosewood City]] ([marked for investigation](marked%20for%20investigation.md)), and
the [[Rosewood Forest]].
Upon setting up camp, we notice a lot of poisonous food. During the first watch, [[Albedo]]
notices a creature of woods emerging out of a tree surrounded by butterflies. It teleports
through the trees towards us and attacks with a butterfly swarm. We defeat them. We finish
our rest.
We travel another half day northeast and we reach the foot of the mountain.
