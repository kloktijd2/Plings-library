[Fishfolk](Fishfolk.md)
- A sharklike fishfolk that are native to a water dimension. They occupied the [Resin Creek](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Resin%20Creek.md) River for a long time.
- When blood is smelled they entered into a maddening ragelike state.
- They are able to have priests and commanders, their priests seem magically powered and the commanders have 4 strong arms.

Settlements
- [Sahuagin Lake](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Sahuagin%20Lake.md) and [Sahuagin Fortress](Sahuagin%20Fortress) as the upper right corner of the river. 
- Originate from a larger settlement in the water dimension called [kraken keep](kraken%20keep). Its currently being attacked by a [kraken](kraken).

Religion
- It seems that they worship an eellike creature dubbed by Lady [Frill](ZZ%20idk%20yet/Frill.md) as: [Moon Eating Morray](Moon%20Eating%20Morray).
- They desire to flood the world so this eal can eat the moon.
