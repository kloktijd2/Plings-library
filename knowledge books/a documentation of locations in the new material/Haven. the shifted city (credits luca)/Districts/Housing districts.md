Multiple districts of varying lifestyles dot the verge of Haven. There is a slum, a couple modest districts and then there is the one where the wealthier of Haven live. 

The slums of the city have been improved slightly by the efforts of the [Chromatic Rose Charity](Chromatic%20Rose%20Charity). Programs to feed the hungry and shelters for the homeless are among the things they have accomplished over the last year under the lead of Strider [Frill](ZZ%20idk%20yet/Frill.md) who took over from Strider [Rakita](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Rakita.md) after her passing at the hand of the [Skarn Wyvern](knowledge%20books/why%20dont%20you%20fix%20this,%20a%20collection%20of%20problems%20by%20pling/Skarn%20Wyvern.md).

Only a couple people live in the wealthy district as most of it is occupied by just a few mansions. These include the [Hargreave Family Estate](Hargreave%20Family%20Estate) and the estate of [Silas Edwards](Silas%20Edwards) and his adoptive daughter [Laowai](Laowai%20Xhê.md). In one the modest districts an orphanage can be found by the name of [Graybeard Orphanage](Graybeard%20Orphanage). 

On the other side of [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md) in the other modest district is Haven’s premier inn, the Last Call Tavern. Most come here to either drown their sorrows and forget their problems or to celebrate the life they still have.
