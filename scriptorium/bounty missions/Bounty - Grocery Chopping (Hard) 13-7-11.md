My fellow companions; [Oxyotl](Oxyotl.md), [Pling](Pling.md), [Orchid](Orchid.md) and I met up with [chef Madeleine](chef%20Madeleine.md) to get the specifics of what we needed to bring back to her. She informs us of the existence of a fruit called [prismarine fruit](knowledge%20books/food%20of%20the%20new%20material/prismarine%20fruit.md). The fruit is red and blue of color, about the size of a [melon](melon) and tastes and smells incredibly sweet. As it can be found around [Makutu Woods](North%20West%20Makutu%20Woods.md), we used a [teleportation circle](teleportation%20circle.md) around [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md) to teleport to [Tall Spire](Tall%20Spire.md). It was HOT and HUMID. The weather was killing me so badly, we even saw a big [fire tornado](fire%20tornado.md) in the distance. From there on out we traveled 4 hours east to reach the edge of  [Makutu Woods](North%20West%20Makutu%20Woods.md). It was raining and the atmosphere felt heavy with magic. There was an overabundance of it swirling around and appearing as dancing lights all around us. [Orchid](Orchid.md), however, managed to figure out that the magic didn’t feel [fey](fey.md) like. After walking for a while longer we encountered a floating blue hand. Orchid’s knowledge helped us again and she was able to recognise that it was a [living spell](living%20spell). We had no choice to engage in combat as it had already noticed us. The hand managed to grab [Pling](Pling.md) and floated up about 50 feet with them in its crushing grip. We made it out alive but that luck wouldn’t last long. We kept going, although a bit on edge after the last encounter. In my [bear](bear) form I managed to track down the sweet smell of the fruit we needed. It was in the trees that were 100 feet tall! Luckily as a [bear](bear) I could easily climb up the tree, [Pling](Pling.md) even rode on my back to help pick the fruit. When we got there however there were giant sloth-like creatures guarding the fruit. I should have known it wouldn’t be as easy as just climbing a tree and picking the fruit. After some negotiation (and mild threatening…) we managed to convince them to do a trade. We searched for fruit in the area and in return they would give us 1 [prismarine fruit](knowledge%20books/food%20of%20the%20new%20material/prismarine%20fruit.md) Although it was getting late so before we made the trade we set up camp and decided to rest. In the middle of the night [Orchid](Orchid.md)’s alarm that she set up went off. A swarm of insects had gravitated towards the fruit and started eating it. We tried to fight them off but it ended up in [Pling](Pling.md) and [Orchid](Orchid.md) getting severely wounded. I thought they were about to die…And Pling actually did. We managed to save Orchid from death but [Pling](Pling.md) succumbed to her injuries from the insects. We made sure to treat her body with care and took her along with us to get brought back by the skilled healers back in [Haven](Haven.md). The next day we made the trade with the sloths and managed to secure the most plump and juicy fruit within their collection. The sweet smell was intense though so we looked for a small stream nearby and covered it in compost to mask the smell. Thank god that was enough to not attract any more beasts in this area. And with that we started the journey back home. I wish I could say everything went smoothly after that but that wasn’t the case… We encountered another [living spell](living%20spell) that caused the gravity to become reversed. It was a very strange feeling to fall towards the sky, I wouldn’t recommend trying it…due to [Orchid](Orchid.md)s quick thinking we didn’t plummet 50 feet down when the spell finally ended. While we walked we saw the big [fire tornado](fire%20tornado.md) again, this time a three headed [Dragon](Dragon.md) flew out from it though. While it was flying around it gave off an extremely blinding light that hurt our eyes, but luckily it flew farther away and not towards us. The heat from the [fire tornado](fire%20tornado.md)was surrounding us, spiraling around us even. That is when it turned into a [fire elemental](fire%20elemental) and we had to defend ourselves once more. It was a tough fight but the three of us managed to tame its fire and got back home safely.
