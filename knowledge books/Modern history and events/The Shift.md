Description: 
[[The Prime Material]] was an old realm, made and maintained by a singular, powerful deity.

[[Ëlun]].

For eons, it sacrificed much of itself to protect this Realm from extraplanar entities, like [[fiends]], [[fey]] and [Abbarations](Abbaration.md). It let its subjects and the other inhabitants of the Prime govern themselves. 

Suddenly, the realm began to collapse. 

With the same zeal it bought time for its subjects to find a solution and save themselves. As the five nations toiled, [[Ëlun]] began to fade, unraveling as the realm cracked open. Natural disasters ravaged the corners of the world and incursions of unspeakable, ravenous abominations plagued the land. With the passing of [the prime](The%20Prime%20Material.md)’s founder, so decayed the Realm.

"Many remember the day ten years ago, an unimaginably beautiful lightshow filled the sky, illuminating the night as if it were day. This celestial event lasted several weeks. Unfortunately, oracles and soothsayers spoke of the realm's doom. Man began to prepare for the disasters that would soon be upon them. Nations set their differences aside, hoping to learn from each other and find methods to save anything and anyone. Time pressed, they managed to articulate an arcane formula capable of encompassing dozens of cities. These would be torn out of time and space, to be jettisoned into an unknowable dark beyond the firmament. This with the goal of entering a suitable realm. For an uncertain time, we were pilgrims in a vast emptiness. Traveling for ages but remembered as a flash. Then, we arrived at a world not unlike our own. We call it the New Material."

  

- An old storyteller

  

Since the magic ritual was never sufficiently refined, it came as no surprise something went wrong in the [[Plane Shift]]. Some attribute ‘the mistake’ to the mages that lent their power, or perhaps a strange interference was to blame? Alas… Instead of all important designated cities being transported, only one district on the fringes was transported. Not a city center with the brightest minds of the former age, but the unfortunate masses. The edge of [Halycion](Haven.md). After ten years, it is still a mystery to everyone what happened.

  

The native gods of this realm took pity on us. They assured us that they saved what they could as we beamed through the planes, and deposited us in what they call "[[The Fallow Lands]]". A land long ruined and abandoned, still visibly scarred by forgotten predecessors and civilizations with similar fates as ours. We were in complete disarray, and as foreigners the essence of this land rejected us. Without the interference of its gods we would have truly suffered before our end. At the behest of [[Calaryn]], fields were instantly plowed and fertilized. With the strength of [[Kravaír]] our fighters repelled the fiercest of beasts. Through the blessings of [[Gescath]], the cycle of life and death was kept intact. With the guidance of [[Ifahmos]], the deceased could find their way to another afterlife. We now honor them, but not everyone shared this sentiment. What remained of [Halycion](Haven.md) - now [[Haven]] - was vulnerable. Some chose to listen to other beings or strive for independence. They left soon after we arrived. There was no one to stop them.

  

Guard Captains without post and exiled nobles quickly cobbled together the [Strider’s Guild](Striders), which returned a semblance of stability and law. This organization would also enable all of its members to directly aid in the defense of [[Haven]] and exploration of the [New Material](New%20Material.md). The era of [[Ëlun]]’s Prime came to an end. We decide the direction of the next.
