Descritption: the [[Dreglord depths]] also known as the depths is the city of the dreglord goblins. it is fully in a crater with at its center deep down [The Great Maw](Great%20Maw.md) (or at least there used to be) The Dreglords seem to be very devout in their beliefs that this being is their god. Showering it with gifts of gems, magical items, and living beings. They look up to their god and devout their city in its glory. Carving their city in the crater sides and even going as far as to make an entire palace hanging above the crater.

Culture:
As a [Dreglord Goblins](Dreglord%20Goblins.md), your life in the city really depends on your position on the social ladder. Those at the bottoms rarely leave the crater. They work mostly in the many mines that delve for [purple crystals](Great%20maw%20crystals.md). These crystals seem to have a chaotic energy that influences the magic around it. This makes it so that the entire crater is constantly fluctuating with magical energy, and as a consequence the Dreglords do not have many casters amongst their society. 

The middle class see the daylight more, living on higher levels and often going outside the crater as aides and assistants to the nobility. They work various jobs in all shapes and sizes and can some days go to bed hungry, and on other celebrate their god with feasts and debauchery. 

The nobility is the class that we know most off. They are treacherous, always trying to get one over each other. Contradictingly they are also very much loyal to the system. Change almost never trickles down. Its these nobility that are entrusted with various tasks, expeditions, and meetings with other factions.
