## Shopping District

There are multiple different shops here. A wide variety of shops include:

- Buy the Way (General Store)
    
- Burning Fork (Adventurer Supplies)
    
- Mad’s Kitchen (Alchemist)
    
- The Enchanted Emporium (Magic)
    
- Ironclad Forge (Smith)
    
- Silken Threads (Tailor)
    
- Radiance Boutique (Temple Shop)
    
- The Thirteenth Carat (Jeweler)
    
- Melodies & Marvels (Music / Games)
    
- Paws and Claws (Pets / Vehicles)
    
- The Creaky Pauldron (Food / Beverages)
