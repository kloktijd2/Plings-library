report on the [Gnoll](Gnoll) Gates expedition written by Khan Bloodhand May 2025

(Transcribed by Guild Clerck [Laowai Xhê](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Laowai%20Xhê.md))

  

We were sent several days south, into the thick jungles of the [Makutu woods](Makutu%20woods), a ruined old fortress is a day and a half southwest of the [Tall Spire](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Points%20of%20intrest/Tall%20Spire.md). When we arrived, we concluded quickly that the fortress was filled to the brim with the vile pests. 

  

We concluded several facts: the [gnolls](knowledge%20books/animals%20of%20the%20new%20material/gnolls.md) were able to multiply their numbers in a rapid timespan. Several missions ago, we had seen several fortresses destroyed. In the northern [Howling Woods](Howling%20Woods), the undead and [demonic](demonic) tribes had already suffered heavy attacks, destroying their fortresses by both our hands and the hands of others. 

  

This fortress was of the tribe that showed no tendency towards [fiends](fiends) or the [undead](undead). They showed only power trough savagery and primal carnage. From the numbers we found it seems that these were the strongest of the three tribes. However we found an even worse truth in the camp. Representatives of both the [undead](undead) and the fiendish [gnolls](knowledge%20books/animals%20of%20the%20new%20material/gnolls.md). These creatures do not only have the common sense to band together between tribes but also to fight together to eachother’s strength. This spells grave news if all 3 are joining in alliance for dark purpose.  
  
What we found is that these [primal gnolls](primal%20gnolls) fight mostly relying on sheer numbers and savagery, although they possess quite a strong number of both warriors and ranged hunters it seemed that the only spellcasters and summoners were from the other tribes.

  

This battle also gave us an insight in another enemy. The [Skarn Wyvern](knowledge%20books/why%20dont%20you%20fix%20this,%20a%20collection%20of%20problems%20by%20pling/Skarn%20Wyvern.md), that blasted beast appeared at the battle. Commanded and puppeteered by the traitor strider [Wycian](Wycian) who killed [Striders](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Striders.md) earlier. While he killed multiple [gnolls](knowledge%20books/animals%20of%20the%20new%20material/gnolls.md), they had to retreat after I personally took interest in killing them. [Wycian](Wycian) once more showed his cowardice colors by retreating with his tail between his legs.

  

After the battle was concluded and the fortress was claimed we spotted a strange monkey who kept their eye on us from the distance, even the ever vigilant [Vivian](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Vivian.md) had difficulty spotting and keeping track of them. Apart from this monkey there were also members of a [thieves guild](thieves%20guild) that is familiar with [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md), they tried to rob us disguised as priest but they were quickly dealth with and executed in the name of [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md) law.
