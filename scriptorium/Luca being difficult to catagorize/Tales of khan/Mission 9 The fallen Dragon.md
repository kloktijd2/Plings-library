report on the keep on following expedition written by [Khan Bloodhand](Khan.md) May 2025

(Transcribed by Guild Clerk [[Laowai Xhê]])

  
  

We travelled outside of [[Haven]] with a powerful but small team: Myself, [[Roboros]] Scourge of the Fishfolk, and the most prominent wizard in Haven, [[Märchen]]. 

Our objective was clear and unknown at the same time. Just like last time ([pling note](pling%20note.md): as far as i am aware there is no last time)we would follow [[Gambker]]’s unwavering pull. Whatever this blade demanded of me it was far up North-West. 

  

 The first couple of days were easy and unnoteworthy. We followed [the creek](Resin%20Creek.md) and quickly and easily dispatched of any threat making its way towards us. With one exception. One of these cultists of the false god (Most likely referring to priest of [[Iyaster]].) made her way with both across the recently cleared river. Typical, not working for its purpose but profiting from it when all the fighting is done. Worse enough its that one that used to be a [[strider]] but gave up after the threat of death became clear. If you can’t handle the heat, don’t enter the kitchen. [[Märchen]] tactfully went in to talk alone while me and [[Roboros]] were out hunting. 

  

It seems [[cultists]] aren’t the only enemies that suddenly come up to attack. When camping in the [[Juran Tallwood]], we were ambushed by a pair of [[devils]]. Howeve,r unlike the followers of that lying entity, these were clear in their origin and their purpose. [[Demons]] sent to me by the [[Crimson Lady]]. What a surprisingly pleasant encounter it was after all the subtrefuge and deceit that lately plagued my missions. One of the [[Devils]] seemed to be almost dancing, taking great pleasure in the blood it pulled from its victim. After attacking me occasionally, it moved on to [[Märchen]], a dreadful mistake. I crushed its spine soon after. The second [[devil]] was a large creature capable of advanced magic and powerful sight. These [[devils]] seem to be able to play into my curse it found out after its attempt of infiltration in [[Haven]]. It made the hunger call to me.

  

After this fight, we continued our trek. A day west and a day north made us reach a mountainous terrain. From the large vantage points we were able to see the entrances of caves, [[Driders]], [[oozes]], and other depth creatures were spotted. 

Not long after we were visited by what appeared to be a female [[Giant]] Chieftess riding ontop of a huge bird. She claims that the creatures are being called from the depths, or rather pushed from below. She told us her clan was nearby, however we had no time to search for that. 

  

The sword guided us down into the caverns. After a small time exploring we found an old civilisation buried underneath the mountain. It was dusty and the pathway outside has not been trodden for some time. This seemed to be a small settlement complete with tavern and even a large monument. A statue towards what appeared to be a gemlike [[dragon]], so whatever lived here must have worshipped them. [[Kobolds]] most likely. 

  

Our price was on the chest of a [[Dark Dwarf]]. An armour of exquisite make. Sturdy and durable but showing large signs of aging, exactly what we sought. After a brief diplomatic talk a fight became clear. I’d say I used self defence but that would be a lie. We were the aggressors and took from them, however this is as it is for the martial communities that I know. In the fighting we were subjected to the powerful magic of the priestess and we lost [[Roboros]]. A glorious loss of life that I repaid 3fold.
