Mission report - Grocery Chopping
Party: [Catherina](Catherine.md) – [[Jean]] – [[Joen]] - [[Gudd]]
Objective: Getting a berry bush plant safely to the cooks.
We all gathered in [[Haven]], my first mission too, very exciting to meet new people
in life. We got the mission to find wild berries, special berries. We need to bring
the full plant safely to the chefs too.
We went in the [[Everplains]], it was a very clear sky today, went North-East to
it. I tried to get to know my allies. And there was a specific one that took my
interest too. [[Catherine]], or [[Cat]] in short, gave me a feeling that she’s shy which I
want to try to help her not shy away.
I also met [[Jean]] and [[Marcel]], what a lovely pig. Then we have [[Gudd]], who is good
like his name.
We found a group of [[goblin]]s yelling at a few people. We decided to intervene
and found out that they killed their gods ([pling note](pling%20note.md) : see [Expedition report Hopefully no mutiny 1](Expedition%20report%20Hopefully%20no%20mutiny%2013-07-26.md) and [Hopfeully no mutiny 2 ](Expedition%20report%20Hopfeully%20no%20mutiny%2013-07-26.md)). this is my fault). We then convinced them that they
could replace their gods and left us, the other party seemed to be fellow striders.
They offered us a quick break, but we declined saying that we needed to head
back to our quest.
Later, we found a [[worg]] running in the wild. With my bear trap and teamwork, [goblins](goblins)
we killed it with ease. Walking bit more, there were some dead [goblin](goblin.md)s, out of
respect we buried them all and gave them something special.
After long walk, we found the hole where there is the berry bush. [[Gudd]] and [Cat](Catherine.md)
went inside with rope, but [[Gudd]] got stuck. We heard from [[Gudd]] that there
were bugs hitting his shell, which was not good. With one move from [[Gudd]] he
finally got free and we all got them out.
[[Jean]] thought quickly and used his utensils to get some ingredients for a
concoction that the bugs won’t like at all. Which worked perfectly! The insects
were gone from the bush, and we safely removed it from the ground.
We decided to make camp and when [[Gudd]] woke us up because he saw a figure
coming closer… And closer… And closer… We started to attack. [[Gudd]] almost
got kidnapped by the creature, we couldn’t make up what it was. Long claws
and blood in the mouth. In the end we killed it and saved [[Gudd]].
As we walked back, we found that the place where we buried the goblins had
blood, a gem that looked like it was form a necklace and a sword all on the
ground … someone was here. We followed the path and it led to [[Resin Creek]].
[[Cat]] quickly made a carrier for [[Marcel]] to carry the bush.
We found a cave, with creatures: a [[worg]], [[hobgoblin]] with a few other [[goblin]]s.
Me, [[Gudd]] and [[Cat]] were hiding in the trees, but it wasn’t long until [[Cat]] was
down, [[Jean]] healed her, but literally after that they killed [[Jean]]… And [[Gudd]]
followed him… It was me and [[Cat]] left… We had to take them all down.
[[Cat]] took care of the last few goblins that were alive and I took care of the [[worg]]
and the [[hobgoblin]]. With my eldritch blast… me and [[Cat]] finished off
everything…
Grief and sadness filled us, yet I tried to stay strong and not show much at all.
We carried the bodies and the bush safely back to [[Haven]] to finish our quest.
