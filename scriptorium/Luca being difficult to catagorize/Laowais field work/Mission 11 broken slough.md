We Travelled from [[the Verge]], having heard reports of a flying ship that seemed almost “unsinkable” from normal weapons piqued the interest in some. While I would love to feel what it would be like to captain a vessel, others were lured by the talks of its magical origin. Especially [[Rudy]] seemed to believe some undead presence might be at the ship.

  

Moving north we were forced into one of the most despised places on this new plane, at least to me. The marshes of the [[Broken Slough]]. Some would argue that my snakelike self should be well adapted to these places and while I agree I do not worry for decease or the poisoned water, I despise the idea of being in these horribly muddled waters for more than I have to. My darling [[Yormung]], my giant constrictor snake, carried me over the waters. 

  

We continued unopposed, only the others seemed to have some issues with the water but nothing noteworthy. For future adventurers it is advisable to find a way to deal with this, perhaps a rowboat or the sort.  

  

At the end of the first day we spot during the night 2 points of lights in the distance. However we did not choose to investigate for the safety of our own. We continued traveling and on the night of the second day we were attacked by a famed Black knight, according to previous reports this was a [[gescathian]] paladin that made some demonic pact to keep doing his work on top of a nightmare. The ability to move into other realms really saved it and doomed our party as we were forced to hide from its dark blade. (See report [[I-Shelan-P]]). We suggest to send the brute here to finally deal with this thing.

  

We continued and spot a small lake we would name [[Torchlight Lake]].  On closer inspection I took initiative to check it out, knowing I could be back with the party the quickest. We found 2 electrical spirits around several bodies in the water. Knowing how useful corpses are to [[Rudy]] and that these bodies probably still had some valuables on them, I was able to discuss with these spirits that, in turn for doing them a service (ooc [[Lao]] left a message in the [[Saber hall]] about a lost wagon of a noble house falling into this pool. Urging people to respect the dead and not go loot them. Ergo those who enter the pool in search of loot would meet their end thus only dooming scum.), I would be spared together with me taking some bodies away so that the pool seemed less dangerous. 

  

From here on out the water seemed eerily poisonous and it is highly advisable to remain submerged for long stretches of time. We sleep once more due to the hard conditions of moving trough this terrain.

We than finally arrived at the river, a small harbour warehouse and docks in a ruined state alongside several dams of debris. We believe the ship would moor here during the evening. To lure the ship out [[Rudy]] used illusion to make a big treasure seem there. Out came the ship with a dark aura around it. [[Undead]] crew made their way to the treasure as [[Albedo]] and I moved onto the ship to battle with the captain. A fight ensued and the Ship seemed alive, capable of hitting us with the anchor. 

  

We disposed of the death and purified the ship, a crew would be send to repair it for our missions. In the cabin we found that it was originally a vessel of a prime faction called the [[Copper Sharks Crew]]. We also found a stone of sending linked to their captain, we would investigate more later.
