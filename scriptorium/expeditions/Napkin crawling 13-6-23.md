Napkin Crawling Expidition
A report by [Natasha Pyoterisk](Natasha%20Pyoterisk), First of her Name
and Trainee of [Leona](Leona%20Bluefall.md)
We went through wicked corpse to the napkin gang base. We
encountered bodies hanging from trees on our travels. A primal
fear envelops us, and [Achillea](Achillea.md) runs away.
[Kurze](Kurze.md) goes after her, and we split up. During this, we get
attacked by a bunch of fiends. Me and [Wynnstan](Wynnstan%20Blackcrown.md) get attacked by an
[insectoid](insectoid) with four arms and a trident. This one seemingly is
resistant against elemental attacks like fire, lightning and
cold.He also looks like he is immune to poison and acid, since he
had something akin to acid dripping from his mouth. He does get
hurt by normal weapons, seemingly having no special resistance
against those.
[Achillea](Achillea.md) and [Kurze](Kurze.md) got attacked by flying fiends, and they quickly
dispatched them. Me and [Wynnstan](Wynnstan) weren't as lucky, the insectoid
fiend envelopping us in a poison cloud. We managed to dispatch
that one and encountered the [Purple Napkin Gang](Purple%20Napkin%20Gang.md)’s, hereby called
[PNG](PNG), Hideout.
The [PNG](PNG)’s hideout was abandoned, with seemingly no human traces
left. [Achillea](Achillea.md) investigated the big building, and encountered an
unconscious man with a napkin and a glyph of warding. I shot the
guy in the chest with my longbow, triggering the glyph from a safe
distance. We then go and head down the stairs.
During the 7 hours of walking, me and [Wynnstan](Wynnstan) recite poetry in
[Dwarvish](Dwarvish). After those 7 hours of walking, we see a purple light
and we enter The [Underdark](Underdark), located right beneath the [PNG](PNG)’s
hideout. We see cows with lights around them moving boxes, 4 [PNG](PNG)
members and two Monstrosities with bird heads and hooks instead of
wings.
We enter combat, there is one leader type [PNG](PNG) member and a duelist
with two scimitars (important for later). During combat, the
duelist gains a new face (I will save you guys the details). We
defeat them and look around the boxes. We encounter bunnies, and
we theorise they use animal carcasses for the duelists to form the
bodies in some way.
After looking around some more, we encountered a plateau in a big
room. In this room, there stand 5 humanoid statues on a platform.
Every statue has a different colored napkin. You have the [PNG](PNG), a
green checkered napkin, an RNG (Red-colored napkin gang), a white
napkin gang and a black napkin gang. There is a small gem in the
statue, and space which makes it seem that it’s able to get moved.
There is also a cage hanging, with a [manticore](manticore) inside. this one
violently trashing against the cage, and seemingly not being able
to talk which normally they should. There are boats in canals, and
big fungi tunnels.
After a bit of exploring, we have a few of those duelists and
another special guy it seems. We did not have time to figure out
what they exactly were. The duelists gain 15 reach and become
monstrous when bloodied, seemingly growing bigger. We flee this
space with our loot.
Please, if you are a powerful [Strider](templates/Strider.md), come with us here. We can
utilize your skills for exploring and mapping. May the serpentine
gods have mercy
