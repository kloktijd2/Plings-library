The rural area outside of [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md) is dedicated to agriculture. The district is made up of small farms and homesteads, with fields as far as the eye can see stretching far and wide. A wide assortment of crops and pastures for livestock assures the survival of Haven’s residents. About a year ago, the district was ravaged by the [Skarn Wyvern](knowledge%20books/why%20dont%20you%20fix%20this,%20a%20collection%20of%20problems%20by%20pling/Skarn%20Wyvern.md) as it had decided to lair next to [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md) and make the area around its hunting grounds. Only through sheer determination and the temporary loss of two more [Striders](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Striders.md) was the beast finally felled allowing the district to slowly return to normal.

Now just about two months ago, the city but mostly the farms again fell prey to a hideous monster. This time it was a [red dragon](knowledge%20books/animals%20of%20the%20new%20material/Dragons/red%20dragon.md) born from underneath [The Verge](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/The%20Verge.md) that was unleashing its fury on the farmlands as the inner city of [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md) had proven itself well defended. Only through the intervention of the [Blue Dragon](Blue%20Dragon.md) was the red one finally driven away.
