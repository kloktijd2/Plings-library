I met up with [Märchen](Märchen%20Obsidianwell.md) at the [[Southwestern gate]]. The goal of today's expedition was to inspect the cause of an incident that happened at the [[Guild hall]]. A [[Strider]] ran into the [[guild hall]], he was no longer amongst the living but a husk of a corpse carried forth only by the dedication to deliver the message. A blue moss had covered his body in no time and we had to quickly dispose of the body with the help of druids. The note he left behind reads as follows.  
"I recently went to the [[Temple of The Sleeper]] to get a closer look, and it seems almost like the ground is raising. Whenever I pass by it on scouting trips, the ground under it gets higher and higher. It's almost perched on a hill! After entering the temple my foot tot snatched by some weird looking moss, though I was able to cut myself free to escape. I don't know what's going on over there but it seems to have made me quite ill so I will no longer be able to investigate myself"

[[Märchen]] has been down there before, when exploring the tunnels created by the [[Skarn Wyvern]], she found a large area filled with a wide variety of mosses. Although it's far-fetched, the moss could have weakened the structural integrity of the already unstable tunnels, which would cause the temple to sink. We also know some of these are very deadly.

Most [[Strider]]s were sadly occupied, so a skeleton crew of [[Märchen]] for her knowledge and I for my natural resistance against poison were selected. We moved at dawn to the [[Skarn Tunnels]]. I'd rather not speak of what we encountered down there, nothing bad, mind you, but the dust. I am a reptillian meant for the sun and water, this was horrible, [[Märchen]] seemed less bothered. The tunnels were safe and uneventful, the dust is really the only uncomfortable thing but it might be a clever idea to fully clear them of any dangers and set up permanent routes.

We eventually reached a cave in, according to [[Khan]]'s maps this would have been near the [[Sleeper temple]]. Moving through we saw rays of light coming from tunnels leading to the surface, these were not man dug and looked like slits within the earth. As we continued we found some sort of cave. It was difficult to walk. Rocky unstable terrain filled by a thick layer of moss and mould. The cave would be lighted by illuminating blue crystals. The air was thick and acidic, there were natural water springs nearby filled with sulfur. We found on our path large thick mushrooms, when interacted with they grew eyes and with some sort of alien clicking noises they communicated with each other. [[Märchen]] quickly established that these must be linked in some sort of hive mind. They did not seem hostile and we continued.

The fog got thicker and the mushrooms only grew in number. From this point [[Märchen]] had already made the connection to what we were walking. [[Myconids]]. I only heard stories of them as a child, how large mushrooms corrupted children lost in the forest and dark. They were tall and varied in their form, but most were thin and riddled with mushrooms and bad backs.

However they were not hostile and welcomed us to the village where more of them were. They looked in awe at us, must not often get guests. It seemed their settlement was build on the sunken ruins of the [[Sleeper temple]]. We eventually met the Village elder, we were not able to pronounce his name so we went with [[Chick'in]] (he was a Chicken on the woods). In the middle of discussion we were stung and paralyzed. When we woke up we were locked up in a cage and he explained that food had been getting an issue. They can only eat rotting dead things and can't go into sunlight. They have to prey on the very few people that stumble in here, hence the two [[strider]] prisoners. Those infected will eventually turn to food.

But we understood that this chief did it for his people than for ill will. We were quickly able to be released using our charms in the matter, explaining that a lot more food can be managed to get a trade route than to kill us and risk [[Märchen]]'s boyfriend finding out. To show our resolve we brought food from the surface and killed some threatening [[great moles]]. They were very pleased with us.

To end our visit we were able to take a nice relaxing day in their sulfur hot springs. It wasn't the most pleasant smell but my skin never felt so smooth. We both gotten luxury gifts for our services to them and they promised to continue the good relations. We were later allowed deeper to find a large crystal surrounded by sphores, they seem to worship this as their true leader calling it ‘[[IT]].’
