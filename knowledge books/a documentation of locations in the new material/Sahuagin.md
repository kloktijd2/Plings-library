[[Fishfolk]]
- A sharklike fishfolk that are native to a water dimension. They occupied the [[Resin Creek]] River for a long time.
- When blood is smelled they entered into a maddening ragelike state.
- They are able to have priests and commanders, their priests seem magically powered and the commanders have 4 strong arms.

Settlements
- [[Sahuagin Lake]] and [[Sahuagin Fortress]] as the upper right corner of the river. 
- Originate from a larger settlement in the water dimension called [[kraken keep]]. Its currently being attacked by a [[kraken]].

Religion

- They desire to flood the world so this eal can eat the moon.
