Report on the [[giants]] clan of the [[Myrrnardir]] and their chieftain [[Ottask]].

(Transcribed by Guild Clerck [[Laowai Xhê]])

  
  

We met with the ‘young’ [[Ottask]]. One of the candidates to become chieftain of the [[Myrrnardir]] tribe, far north. 6 days, meaning after the mountains controlled by the Heralds of Ire. On our way there, [[Barrek]] had given him the headband of intellect. It appears this one seemed weaker than the competition, but a lot more compassionate. However, compassion and kindness are not values that are sought after in his tribe. We had to prepare him for trials to choose the next chieftain. A tournament will be held, and [[Ottask]] was a candidate. From what we learned later, it would seem that [[Ottask]] is the only one that would be an ally with Haven.

  

On the way there, each one of us learned him a valuable skill.  [[Ek]] taught him the possibilities of his craft. This was almost only possible with the power of the headband, but with tinkering, he was able to infuse and create. [[Barrek]] taught him the power of animalistic totems. If I recognised them correctly, he learned the power of the [[Aries]]. [[Märchen]] would teach him the power of wielding magic, although he only learned low-level magic the way he did seemed to be interesting.  

  

On the way we would have found a number of different tombs. Each corresponds with a version of [[giants]] with a legendary warrior buried deep. These tombs were sealed with powerful runic magic and require a password to open. [[Märchen]] decided to explore the fire tomb however, the other seemed to be unopened and may have magical artefacts inside.

  

Lastly, I taught [[Ottask]] the mystical power of Runecrafting, using one’s body to influence the magic. Trusting in the power inside rather than outside. When closing our destination we saw each the same bloodied and powerful dream. [[Ottask]] had found and activated a magical circle, seemingly one of transportation. However this one still needs to be set correctly for us to use. ([pling note](pling%20note.md) : see also [teleportation circle](teleportation%20circle.md))

  

We arrived at the clan grounds 6 days north. This was clearly a camp of [[hill giants]]. Some possessed magical capabilities of some sorts but these were highly in the minority and mostly due to items. Although they would be a problem as enemies, they would not be undefeatable. 

During the tournament, our party cheated with magic to push the favor to [[Ottask]]. I disagreed with this move but eventually we won. The chief surrendered his role to [[Ottask]] and adding us a new ally. However would [[Ottask]] have been able to win without our help. What if another challenger appears and we are not there to help him.
