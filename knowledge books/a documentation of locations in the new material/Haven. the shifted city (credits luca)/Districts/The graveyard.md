The newest graves are arranged haphazardly, with overgrown paths and crooked headstones adding to the feeling of disorientation and unease. In the chaos of [[The Shift]] a lot of people died and they were all buried here. Most of the original graveyard was destroyed when it landed on the [[Nirant Flow]].  As there wasn’t any order to the graveyard, people got buried left and right. There wasn’t anyone left to take care of the graves which saw the graveyard get squandered. Now lately the place has seen a better upkeep, but it’s still far from what it used to be.
