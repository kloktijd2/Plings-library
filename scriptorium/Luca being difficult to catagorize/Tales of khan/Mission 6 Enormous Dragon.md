report on the Bluey Dragon expedition written by [Khan Bloodhand](Khan%20Bloodhand) 28 jan 2025

(Transcribed by Guild Clerck [Laowai Xhê](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Laowai%20Xhê.md)) 

  

We left Haven to the western gate. The target was the fabled [Dragon Mountain](Dragon%20Mountain) in [the creek](Resin%20Creek.md) that was rumoured to be home to the large [Blue Dragon](Blue%20Dragon.md) that had saved Haven before. We wanted to establish speaking terms and try and get on his good side since the attacks came. The way seem to be one already made by a mission some time ago, read the chronicles of adventurer [Lila](Lila). 

  

We traveled with 3 high levelled adventurers westward without little interference on the road. We are to strong to be stopped by Robbers, or [gnolls](knowledge%20books/animals%20of%20the%20new%20material/gnolls.md). After a day we arrived at some ruins where [Frill](ZZ%20idk%20yet/Frill.md) is able to tell us there are live [Stone Giants](Stone%20Giants). I do not know why but perhaps a combination of the search for danger, greed, and a divine judgement caused me and [Roboros](Roboros) to destroy these beings. They are surprisingly strong so do look out. We managed to find several special roses alongside the big gems on the [Giants](Giants) chest. We continued our journey afterwards.

  

Continuing a day west we gotten into a very dense woodland that seemed different from the rest of [the Creek](Resin%20Creek.md). In the dark there were several grey humanoid monkey-esque creatures with dark eyes and blood gushing out of their maw. They were smart to keep their distance while we travelled through these dense woods or I would have added their heads to my shrine. Eventually, as night decent we found ourselves at the foot of the Mountain. The mountain was not the most difficult to climb especially with the aide from tools however what made this climb difficult was the exceedingly strengthening storm. Large waves of rapid lightning strikes occurred and occasionally hit us and huge winds kept us slow. Eventually, we met an elder [bugbear](bugbear). [Frill](ZZ%20idk%20yet/Frill.md) believes this person might have some shapeshifting abilities. They did not seem bothered by the storm that would normally prove detrimental for someone of such age and small build. Later we learned this might be a creature that has syphoned the power of a [dragon Ascension](dragon%20Ascension). Further information about this man’s whereabouts will be rewarded by [Khan](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Khan.md). 

  

Following deeper into the mountains we found a group of blue [kobold](kobold) followers. While I did not understand their chanting it seemed mostly religious and [Frill](ZZ%20idk%20yet/Frill.md) tells me they were awaiting the [ascension](ascension) with great enthusiasm. We quickly had to take cover as a huge amount of electrical power flashed trough the mountains. These tiny [kobolds](kobolds) than became small dragonlings, I believed [Frill](ZZ%20idk%20yet/Frill.md) called them [Wyrmlings](Wyrmlings). This may also be the reason of the rumoured syphoning of the [Dragon](knowledge%20books/animals%20of%20the%20new%20material/Creature%20types/Dragon.md)’s power. 

  
  
  

We continued further and eventually the mountain itself gave up around the power of the [Dragon](knowledge%20books/animals%20of%20the%20new%20material/Creature%20types/Dragon.md). According to [Frill](ZZ%20idk%20yet/Frill.md) it was in the middle of its [ascension](ascension), whatever that meant. Soon the mountain split up in large floating boulders, hanging hundreds of feet in the air connected by lightning. This is where we got attacked by a huge amount of recovering air, lightning and dust spirits. The fight was hectic and eventually with a huge shockwave, we all passed out. 

  

When I recovered I saw before me the largest [Dragon](knowledge%20books/animals%20of%20the%20new%20material/Creature%20types/Dragon.md) ever. A colossal blue beast that landed before us. It shared with us the wisdom it had. Mostly it did not seem aggressive to [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md) and confirmed it had no benefit to its destruction. It seemed to mumble about an age of dragon infighting. It distributed further great wisdom to each of us alongside filling some of our items with the greater power of a dragon. It told us it might be interested in posting quests in the future for when he has a need for us [Striders](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Striders.md). It then allowed us to RIDE ITS FRICKING BACK TO HAVEN! (Khan shouted this). 

  

Overall the [Dragon](Blue%20Dragon.md) seemed to remain neutral if not agreeable with us as long as we remain good allies with the [Wyrmblood Kobolds](Wyrmblood%20Kobolds).
