report on the Call of the Deep mission written by [Khan Bloodhand](Khan.md) Feb 2025

(Transcribed by Guild Clerck [Laowai Xhê](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Laowai%20Xhê.md)) 

  

Transcriber note: When [Khan](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Khan.md) came to me he seemed enraged far beyond his usual state. It appears that he believes the enemies and misfortunes he encountered today were send by the god [Iyastar](Iyastar). He even went as far as call the god a dark fiend that puppeteers people and his report shows his believes quite clearly so I will do my best to identify the troublesome parts. I made sure to keep my door close lest he get in trouble but someone needs to help him, not me though. I’m not paid to be a therapist.

  
  

“We set out Southward to get to those runes of [Runfyr](Runfyr). Apparently there were issues last time we sent [Striders](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Striders.md) there. I heard you got killed over there. Seems we both have issues there. When we arrived, the lake had a ton of ice. More so than the last reports would suggest. Unnatural spikes of ice rose from the water. If that wasn’t a sign enough that something terrible has a hold on that ruin, then the water should make it clear. It was oily and vile. Yet alluring as can be, [Roboros](Roboros) drank, but his stomach couldn’t handle it. Further magic was observed at night. The moon shone as bright as the sun. An ill omen for what was to come, I’m certain of it.”

  

“When we got into the water, the first thing we noticed was that the slime had expanded, covering nearly every surface of the ruins. We kept our guard up as we knew of the [Fishfolk](unsorted/Fishfolk.md) hiding in it. Finding the cave in all the goo was no easy feat. When we entered, we eventually found a rune. It lit up, but to this day, I have no idea what it would do. Deeper we found your previous companions. [Goblinoid](Goblinoid) undeath. They were with the [Fishfolk](unsorted/Fishfolk.md) in these icy waters. It did not take a lot of effort to dispose of them. We afterwards found a weird little [kobold](kobold) in these caves. I do not know where they came from or what they did, but they showed us the way.”

  

“We eventually gotten into a large room where the vile false god made his presence known. A statue to depict their full glory was in the middle of the room, surrounded by all manner of wall paintings, murals, I think the word is. They depicted some sort of history. But I’m sure that whatever they show here is far from the truth. An army of [angels](angels) clashing, a mountain being moved, a city lifted from the ground, a meteor moving down, and a divine figure. We put a weapon and scroll in the hands of the statue. This showed the path to several treasures and what would be a death trap if not for the fact we were underwater. This was the first instance of the false idol showing their true colours.” 

  

“We ended up in front of the cathedral dedicated to this god of law. We first believed this structure to be a place of security as we slept to regain some power. Inside this cathedral, there lay a sword in a stone. We believed it a gift, or a cry for help to be reclaimed to the known world, or perhaps a reward for clearing this holy place. But how wrong we were. [Roboros](Roboros) and I tried to pull the blade out, but only the full extent of my strength was needed to pull it out. When we pulled the blade out, a surge of radiance came from it, and it killed our [kobold](kobold) guide and wounded the rest of us.”

  

“The sword was no gift from god, but a cursed object from a conniving [devil](devil). During the night we were ambushed by waves of the [Ryndel](Ryndel) [Fishfolk](unsorted/Fishfolk.md). The curse that the sword infused in us made [Roboros](Roboros) and I go insane and to attack our loved ones. This was the last straw, we had to retreat from the [demons](demons) hiding in the shadows of the cathedral. I made sure to topple the statue and vowed to return to finish the job.”
