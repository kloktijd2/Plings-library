Mission Report: Cave Diving in the Silent Woods
At the crack of dawn, the party—consisting of [Orchid](Orchid.md), [Sedressel](Sedressel.md), [[Gaven]], and
myself—assembled at the [[guild hall]]. [[Amber]] briefed us on the cave we were tasked to
explore, located a two-day journey northeast of [Haven](Haven.md) within the [[Silent Woods]]. Our first day
of travel was mostly uneventful, save for a bizarre encounter with a cart full of cabbages that
had seemingly plummeted from the sky ([[pling note]]: see also [shift storm](shift%20storm.md)? [Oxyotl](Oxyotl.md) is unsure. apparantly a portal opened up); they were charred and hot to the touch, so we
wisely chose to leave them alone. That night at camp, the sky revealed a strange
anomaly—had the moon always had a crack in it ([[pling note]]: should i  tag this?: [[moon crack]] ? By dusk on the second day, we reached
our destination: a cave entrance situated atop a hill in the [[Silent Forest]]. We opted to set up
camp outside and begin our descent the following morning.
Entering the cave at dawn, we immediately spotted what appeared to be a [[griffon]]'s nest
containing an egg, some scattered bones, and a pile of gold. However, as we approached,
the mirage vanished, revealing an illusion cast by a demon known as [[the Informant]]. The
entity offered a treacherous deal: in exchange for full intelligence on all the dangers ahead, it
demanded the current and future knowledge of one party member. We firmly declined, but
Orchid attempted to bargain. Despite the party's skepticism, she traded all her past
knowledge to learn the location of the "[[crown piece]] quest". The demon was pleased with the
exchange and granted her the information.
Pressing deeper into the cavern for a few hours, we emerged into a massive subterranean
chamber. Before us lay a petrified forest; the trees, grass, and even small animals were
composed entirely of solid stone. Our inspection revealed nothing inherently special about
the rock itself, leading us to surmise it was the result of a petrifying force—one that even
restoration spells would likely fail to reverse. As we navigated the stone woods, we
encountered a bull-like beast. Closer inspection revealed it to be a [[gorgon]], which swiftly
grew enraged and attacked. We managed to defeat the creature, taking careful measures to
avoid its petrifying breath.
Further into the forest, we spotted a massive gate guarded by several stone humanoid
figures. Testing their hostility, we fired a warning arrow from a distance, which immediately
provoked them into a charging attack. During the ensuing skirmish, [[Gaven]] attempted to
reach their minds telepathically but received no response, confirming they were not regular
living beings. After dismantling the guards, we approached the sturdy gate. Unable to open it
conventionally, we used magic to transmute the stone into wood and melted it down with fire.
Beyond the gate lay a sprawling underground city, faintly reminiscent of the ruins we
previously saw near the [Dreglords](Dreglord%20Goblins.md)' domain ( #plingIsInterested ). Prioritizing our primary investigation, we decided
to bypass the city for now and returned to an alternate path we had noticed near the front of
the forest.
This secondary path proved drastically different; after a few hours of walking, the cavern
walls began to gleam and shine with a divine-like radiance, possibly influenced by the region
above ground. Exhausted, we set up camp for the day, but our rest was interrupted just as
we began to eat. Two bizarre, cornucopia-like creatures ambushed us, forcefully attempting
to feed us. Even after indulging them, they relentlessly tried to force-feed us further, pushing
us into a desperate battle. We barely survived the encounter, with [[Gaven]] nearly perishing
from overeating and extreme exhaustion. The rest of the night was miserable as we
recovered—and threw up—from the ordeal.
On the fourth day, we pressed on and found a fork in the path. One route led upward to the
surface, depositing us briefly in the Eastern Woods. We decided to head back down and see
where the remaining tunnels led. Returning to the [[glittering cave]], we eventually reached
another split. Scouting with our familiars, we discovered that one path led to a large
underground lake infested with crocodiles and other aquatic threats, while the other
ascended and carried the foul stench of rotting flesh. Avoiding the [[crocodiles]], we took the
upward path and soon discovered two humanoid corpses. Based on their burst stomachs
and signs of overeating, they had clearly fallen victim to the cornucopia creatures a few days
prior. Examining their gear, we suspected they belonged to the [[New Wings]] faction, though
we could not be completely certain.
Continuing upward for another two hours, we found our exit blocked by a large gate leading
to the surface. Our scouting familiars were immediately shot down, revealing a heavily
fortified bandit or faction settlement just beyond. [[Gaven]] blasted the gate open, exposing a
squad of defending soldiers. We noticed a network of strange vents and attempted to shoot
them down, but their captain shouted that the cavern was already filled with gas. She threw
fire into the area, causing a devastating flash fire that immediately took [[Sedressel]] down.
Overwhelmed by a sudden flood of heavier reinforcements, [[Orchid]]—as the last one
standing—was forced to surrender, as the enemy threatened to execute our downed
companions if we tried to escape.
We were taken prisoner and brought before their captain, an imposing figure clad in full back
armor and wielding specialized gauntlets. She interrogated [[Orchid]] regarding the [Striders Guild](Striders.md)'s intelligence on the [[New Wings]]. Before eventually releasing us back into the cave, the
captain delivered a strict ultimatum to [[Orchid]], warning us never to return to their settlement.
The cost of our release, however, was astronomical. They stripped us of all our belongings,
leaving us with nothing but a single dagger. Tragically, they murdered [[Gaven]], and in a brutal
display of cruelty, severed the hands of both [[Sedressel]] and myself. It is a miracle we
managed to make it back to Haven with [[Gaven]]'s body. While we successfully completed the
investigation of the cave, a bitter taste remains; we lost our most prized possessions to
Haven's enemy, the [[New Wings.]]
