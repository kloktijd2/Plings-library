A plan has been set in motion to take down one of the largest threats near our city. The [Sahuagin](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Sahuagin.md) and us have an uneasy peace that revolves mostly around live and let live etiquette. However, they were a threat, an obstacle to overcome should we want to secure our own access to the waters and allow expansion into the [Creek](Resin%20Creek.md). Thus with the assistance of other adventurers we have concluded a plan on how to defeat them. I and Chief [Khan](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Khan.md) of the the Rockeater Remnants made a plan to take them down, I only have a limited training in military operations but with [Khan](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Khan.md)’s experience we were ready for the assault.  
  
[Khan](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Khan.md) would have convinced the River trolls, the natural enemy of the [Sahuagin](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Sahuagin.md) to join in fight against them. From there a multitude of different groups with their own respective tasks. An infiltration group under my command would move in while the trolls under [Khan](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Khan.md) distracted the [Sahuagin](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Sahuagin.md) alongside our reserve forces under the Strider [Barrek](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Barrek.md). When the infiltration group give the order the main force led by the Head Strider would assault the fortress. 

[Tactical assault of the depth](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1WzJplbEZgv7hcPGziXq8bYpZOKoByLnF5S-__m2NnGw/edit?usp=sharing)

The plan went without a hitch, the [Fishfolk](unsorted/Fishfolk.md) did not see it coming and took the bait. As we infiltrated the fortress via a hidden pathway revealed to us by [the Great Elk](ZZ%20idk%20yet/the%20Great%20Elk.md) it became clear that not all troops left to hunt. When entering the tunnel we found writings of a pirest of [Calaryn](knowledge%20books/world%20religions/Gods/Calaryn.md). We entered the prison, found several creatures and loyalists of [the Great Elk](ZZ%20idk%20yet/the%20Great%20Elk.md), as well as a captured [Fishfolk](unsorted/Fishfolk.md) from [Ryndel](Ryndel), this is concerning for how would they have made contact, [Ryndel](Ryndel) is south several days from here. Must investigate. It does not seem that the [Ryndel](Ryndel) variants can walk above water and the [Sahuagin](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Sahuagin.md) are very territorial. Perhaps an underground river might connect them. The creature teached us much, it serves a serpentine creature which is emitting a strong charming magic over its subjects. We freed it and watched it cause chaos. Inside the prison and further information of what might be an old elven empire. There seems to be a linked to the driders that were recently encountered in the [dripping Waeld](dripping%20Waeld). need to investigate further§§

  

Exploring the depths further, several weaknesses became clear, electricity in the water is a double edged blade. For those who can dispose of electricity with a great control such as my fist will find it especially useful against the enemy, but more chaotic attacks such as lightning bolt might swirl out of coarse. 

  

We found many treasures and [magical artifacts](magical%20artifacts) in this hideout. There seems to be some religious development to that of a [primal kingdom](primal%20kingdom). Golden statues dedicated to their god and their priests. From what we could tell the priestesses were able to cast magic and primarily were the females of the species while the males with 4 arms seemed to be the knights. We disposed of what seemed to be high ranking individuals but found information that more are in the main fort of [Kraken Keep](Kraken%20Keep). There are 7 in total, we took care of about 3 but due to the  chaotic assault we are unsure ofthe exact number. The 7th seems to be their god.

  

While we robbed many rooms and destroyed many things, eventually we had to retreat to save our breath. We have conquered and dealth a fatal blow to the enemy, their occupance of the river is gone, although a tunnel led deeper into the dark. I wish to investigate this one day. To secure our victory we are to raise a settlement at one of the two lakes and defend it from any remnants still remaining. To ease us in full control of the river, [Khan](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Khan.md) had received the order to kill the trolls after they served his purpose. He was not fond of this decision but it must be done.
