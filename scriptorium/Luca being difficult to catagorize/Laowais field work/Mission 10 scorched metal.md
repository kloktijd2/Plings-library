Sir [Oreo](Oreo)

The guild had received reports of a dangerous, and strangely powerful, [bulette](bulette) kicking a fuss in the desert to the north. The way to the [Dry Sea](Dry%20Sea) is a long one and one that is not often, nore easily, made. Not that I have issues with the desert heat. I in fact like it much more than the usual rain or cold seasons we have around here. Preparing by buying enough ways for us to remain hydrated I ensured that the Edward household could supply a small convoy alongside water. From what we know of the desert is that it goes far and deep. But no sign of life had been found as of yet. This information comes from Strider [Shelan](Shelan), the lead scout as of late.

We moved north as the instructions go. The first few days of travel were less interesting so I will not write them in full. The first day we arrived at a [copper statue](copper%20knight%20statue.md) as is often stated. This statue, as is reported, slays foes who have bad intentions. We heard the howling of nearby [gnolls](knowledge%20books/animals%20of%20the%20new%20material/gnolls.md) during the day so in a way of securing our camps more, [Rudy](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Rudy.md) was able to use some of the bodies around this statue to be our protectors.

The second day went along as normal, with camp being made at the [Defanged Tower](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Points%20of%20intrest/Defanged%20Tower.md) which has been pacified fully.

The third day the weather had shifted for the worse, we were beset by much heavier rains and winds. Any tracks of enemies were no longer detectable. 

The Fourth day went by without much trouble, at least until the afternoon. We were beset suddenly by an ambush of a handful of [demon gnolls](demon%20gnolls) and their demonic beasts of war. Making use of the fact we were not able to find their tracks. We fend them off without much trouble but a lower levelled party might have had more issues. We also found a small hut but this had nothing of importance to note.

At the fifth day we have reached the first interesting point. The hills where the multiple [hill giant](hill%20giant) tribes live, with the allied [Ottask](Ottask) tribe being one of them. We travel north for another half a day but due to our scouting, our information from the fallen strider [Rakita](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Rakita.md) (passed on by [Frill](ZZ%20idk%20yet/Frill.md)), and the sight of the mountains we decide to already move North-West after a half a day. This way we avoid the infamous fort [Morne](Morne) where the devotees of the dark god [Kravair](Kravair) lie. 

The sixth day acknowledged our worries, we spot the mountains and the Fort in the distance when we arrived during the evening. Yet luckily it was far enough to be out of reach of their attacks or scouts and we can pass by without an issue. 

At the 7th night we finally arrived at the desert. It was truly magnificent, in a very cruel and lonely way. We camped and experienced the unique trait of the sands. Where the days are as hot as can be, yet the nights can be just as cold. Yet this was a new sight, new lands. I don't necessarily enjoy striding but these moments can even crack a smile with me.

We continued on and after another half a day we found tracks of a creature. Large rifts in the sand as if something has been dragged. Uncertain if it was the result of a [bulette](bulette) we chose to investigate nonetheless. Only to found something just as strange, an incredibly large beetle with some scorpion like features. It seemed to be affiliated with a culture in these sands. 

The next day we arrived at their outpost, an oasis where we refilled our waters, or at least that was the plan but water is used as a currency here. Quite a goldmine for any merchant brave enough to venture this far out of the city. They trade gold and items for it yet their beetle like self’s enjoy droppings too much for me to be a fan. Yet they tell us of a grand city about 10 more days to the east. Certainly something that peaked my interests, if only to show how insane large this desert is.

These people are to be called [Chittering Wanderers](Chittering%20Wanderers) we remained there as guests for another day and a half.

At the dawn of the 10th day we did our last travel into the desert, eventually finding the beast. It was a pushover, a few lucky strikes from me, strong spells from the casters, a critical hit from the ranger, and [Leona](Leona%20Bluefall.md)’s rapid swings brought it down despite it being shielded by a particular object. 

We found it to be an animated shield and not just anything. The runes and aura it omits reminded [Leona](Leona%20Bluefall.md) of those on [Khan](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Khan.md)’s weaponry, we found the last of his items and I am satisfied with the knowledge we can start demanding a kings ransom for finding it from the brute.
