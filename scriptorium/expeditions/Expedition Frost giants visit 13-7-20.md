Frost Giants Visit
[Khan](Khan.md) flies Barrek and I to the [Whistling Peaks](Whistling%20Peaks.md). We couldn’t find [Vivian](Vivian.md) so we
had no choice but to set off without her. We look up at the floating islands
that are drifting atop the clouds. There seems to be endless rainfall coming
from these clouds. I am informed that these islands were named the [Mourning crown](Mourning%20crown). We decide it’s best to let [Khan](Khan.md) fly us up into the clouds, beyond the
rainfall.
The skies are packed with floating ice crystals and dense fog. The visibility
here is so poor that we can only see normally for about 15 feet and another
20-25 feet of extremely blurry sight. The gravity seemed to work just as usual,
thankfully. The wind seems to be blowing in a sort of circling vortex around
us, making it easier for us to navigate depending on where the wind’s
direction is coming from. It is at this point that [Vivian](Vivian.md) joins us, seeing her
on her broom flying towards us was a relief. She was the one that started to
notice shadows around us. Some appeared as birds, only to end up being ice
shards when we moved closer. Others seemed like humanoid shapes that
disappeared with the roar of thunder. We eventually find an area with better
visibility and see that it is a tunnel. We choose to go to the right and travel
in this area for better visibility. The more we travelled through the tunnel
we started to notice that the tunnel was slowly collapsing behind us, as if the
clouds were filling the space again.
The tunnels itself seemed to have a pattern in them, like they were made by
something that was digging through these clouds. The temperature dropped as
we entered what looked to be a chamber made from the interior of a cloud. It is
here we find remnants of a nest, a water basin made from ice and a place with a
snowy mushy layer that looks like a resting place. I find drag marks on the
floor and some more evidence of something taking off. It definitely appeared
that something still lived here. I made an attempt to make tunnels of my own
but the combination of displacing the clouds and making them stay static was
too hard of a combination to keep up.
We wanted to get out of there quickly to avoid the inhabitants coming home, so
Khan flew us all out of there back into the clouds. I managed to hold on but the
wind currents were quite strong. [Barrek](Barrek.md) and [Vivian](Vivian.md) lost their grip and
smashed into each other as they dangled from [Khan](Khan.md). The safety ropes were the
right decision it seemed. This is where I started to feel uneasy. I could feel a
storm approaching as we flew up, luckily I anticipated the lightning strikes
in time so I could absorb a big part of its energy. It seems to be that this area
is made from different layers, each carrying their own dangers. A bit later
[Vivian](Vivian.md) spots a fairly big, flat creature flapping through the clouds. It is too
hard to see fully with the fog but we avoided it successfully. We press on with
[Vivian](Vivian.md) on her broom helping us navigate as I steer [Khan](Khan.md) by the horns on his
helmet where to fly to. While we fly, [Barrek](Barrek.md) is dangling from Khan’s leg while
doing situps. The man truly is fearless.
Then a form of darkness approaches us from above. I steered [Khan](Khan.md) out of the way
to avoid impact but when it got closer it didn’t seem to be solid. [Barrek](Barrek.md)
decided to stick his head in for a bit, apparently it was quite warm and
comfortable inside. When he came back out he had a plate sized egg stuck in his
beard.
Unfortunately, due to the temperature the egg froze over making it impossible
to ever grow life. We decided to take two of the eggs out of curiosity, one was
being carried by [Barrek](Barrek.md) on his chest like a baby sling, the other was carried
by me wrapped in one of Khan’s cloaks, tied to my back. These eggs needed all
the warmth they could get to survive.
We find evidence of life in the form of remnants of campfires frozen deep
inside an iceberg. While I was flying with [Vivian](Vivian.md) on her broom, [Barak](Barak) managed
to crash [Khan](Khan.md) into one of the icebergs. Maybe next time it would be best not to
let [Barrek](Barrek.md) drive [Khan](Khan.md), as much of a funny sight that it was they could have
gotten seriously hurt. Luckily this time the iceberg was more hurt than them
as they managed to nearly split the thing down the middle. Inside was some sort
of fragment of a telescope lens, it was HUGE.
We find a small island where we decide to set up camp for the night. [Barrek](Barrek.md)
managed to fish and successfully caught a sailfish while [Khan](Khan.md) wanted to hunt
for a whale. He didn’t find one, however he managed to lure a cloud ray towards
us. We fought the thing, with [Khan](Khan.md)’s grapple, [Barrek](Barrek.md)’s position on top of it to
ride it, and [Vivian](Vivian.md) and I hitting it from a distance. We made quick work of it.
To all [Striders](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Striders.md) reading it, be careful though. These cloud rays have the
ability to stun you, nearly causing Barrek to plummet to his death if I didn’t
get to him in time. This was also an adult ray, but after inspection we realised
there are even bigger ones roaming here.
[Hyrrokin](Hyrrokin) and the chariot managed to carry us even further up to a small island
where we see 3 buildings. There didn’t seem to be people around there so we
went higher up to an island where we spotted a fortress city. There’s many
buildings like an observatory, a smithy, a weather station, a meeting hall, a
building with foul smelling smoke coming out of it and many more. We see a
[giant](knowledge%20books/Taxonomical%20diffrences%20between%20diffrent%20humanoid%20races/giant.md) setting on the side steps of the observatory so we head there. His name is
[Jorund Sky-Scribe](Jorund%20Sky-Scribe) and he is one of the scientists of the [frost giants](frost%20giants). We make
some friendly conversation and he invites us to the meeting hall to speak more
comfortably. We meet some other giants there and learn that they have been
down on the ground before. [Vivian](Vivian.md) leads the conversation as they exchange
some knowledge. We are treated well, even getting a warm meal and ale to
refuel. [Jorund](Jorund) promised he would propose some kind of diplomacy to the
council, if we could make it worth their while. They seem to be folk with lots
of knowledge already, so having to prove ourselves to gain their trust fully
might prove a difficult task. They supply us with rooms to stay for the night.
The next morning [Jorund](Jorund) informs us that the council accepted our proposal but
that they will formally need to discuss the terms of the diplomacy. For a
second we were glad to have established some sort of an alliance, until it was
mentioned that this process would take at least 4 years. At least we made a good
start. [Jorund](Jorund) tells us a little more about their way of life before we had to
leave. We learned that the frost giants are split into 3 main groups; the [Ice Bound](Ice%20Bound), the [Sky Keepers](Sky%20Keepers) and the [Deep Forged](Deep%20Forged). The [Ice Bound](Ice%20Bound) are the warriors, the
[Sky Keepers](Sky%20Keepers) are the scientists and lastly the [Deep Forged](Deep%20Forged) serve as the crafters.
We also learned the names of the islands. The top island is called [Crown Hold](Crown%20Hold).
You also have a long curved island called [the Shelf](the%20Shelf), sometimes also referred to
as the [Scholar Shelf](Scholar%20Shelf). [The Shelf](The%20Shelf) itself has a smaller island that is positioned a
bit lower that is called the [Storm Wash](Storm%20Wash). The other islands are the [Hollow Crown](Hollow%20Crown), which is a very unstable island, the [Frost Wood](Frost%20Wood), which as the name
suggests is a forest island and the [First Gem](First%20Gem), which is an island covered in
some sort of ruins.
He also spoke of an area that is called the [Weeping Glacier](Weeping%20Glacier), but he was quite
vague about the location of it. Probably because it is the water source for
everything here and there isn’t enough trust yet.
Mapping this area will be quite hard as these islands float around and change
placements. They stay in their respective areas generally but do sway and move
as the winds carry them in certain directions. It was also quite noticeable
that it is harder to breathe up here, anything physical we did felt more
draining as usual.
We did manage to convince the [Sky Keepers](Sky%20Keepers) to be somewhat open to diplomacy,
maybe if we convince the other branches the process will go easier.
