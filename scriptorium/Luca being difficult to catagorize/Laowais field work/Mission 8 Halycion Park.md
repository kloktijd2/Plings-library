Lady Commander [Laowai Xhê](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Laowai%20Xhê.md):  
Lady [Leona Bluefall](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Leona%20Bluefall.md)

Sir [Albedo](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Albedo.md) of the Rose

  
  
  
  

We as always, departed from the gate. The company this time was extraordinarily to my tastes. The honourable and knowledgeable Lady [Leona](Leona%20Bluefall.md) would be our guide. The noble and magnificent Sir [Albedo](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Albedo.md) would be our great scout and hunter. And I, the noble Lady [Laowai Xhê](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Laowai%20Xhê.md) of house Edwards will be the diplomat, chronologer, and finding tracks from the animals. Our task was to find and transport several of these large dinosaurs back to Haven. I had employed my position as a noble to put together a convoy and workers.

  

Several days into our adventure we finally reached the [Juran Tallwood](Juran%20Tall%20Woods.md), for those out of the loop this is a place where ancient lizards frequent. The trees reach the sky, and the blue dragon uses this area as his playground. But to our surprise there was more here than we could have ever realised. 

From the bushes between the giant trees he came, a tall warrior and a rare sight even for our culturally diverse society.  A [lizardman](lizardman), clad in hunter’s attire, markings and scar on his body. And a stone spear at his sight. However for whatever mystery this man brought with him, it was outdone by hostility. As the warrior aimed his weapon at us and believed us enemies, solely because of me. Apparently there were not only [lizardmen](lizardmen) and dinosaurs, but also some [Yuan-ti](Yuan%20ti.md). While I was understandably interested in the prospect of my kin being in this strange world, I did not enjoy this scaled warrior’s aggression to me, a civilised lady. After educating this barbaric savage about my position in the glorious settlement of [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md) we were invited to meet their stronghold, here we could lay official business forward and even get their help in finding dinosaurs for our mission. [Leona](Leona) and [Albedo](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Albedo.md) seemed interested and fairly friendly towards these wildlings, however I would be lying if I did not admit I had my reservations. I finally learned more about the possibility of more of my kind out there, I havent seen many since I left my homeland. 

  

We met deeper into their camp and spoke with their shaman; their welcome of us was cold, well, at least to me. Apparently, they call themselves the [Kecualas Tribe](Kecualas%20Tribe). From what I gather they do not pose a significant treath to our top soldiers. Their soldiers would equal about an intermediate adventurer and their shaman’s might be a higher ranked mage, but nothing that the elites cannot handle in case they become a threat. While we did have the authority to form a non aggression pact, I objected that I first need more information about the different beings in this forest. 

They hunt dinosaurs and recognise them as honourable hunts, while we could have just poached a few beasts like we did before, it is easier for us with their assistance and blessing. But what they spoke further intrigued me, they showed us a very primitive starmap. From it we can see that the [lizardmen](lizardmen) have 4 settlements, and the [Vrael Olo](Vrael%20Olo), their [Yuan-Ti](Yuan-Ti) enemies had only 2 confirmed spots. 

The star map also showed multiple other interesting parts, several ruins and something they call ‘[Luxum](Luxum).’ It has a deposit, although it be nearly empty. But so far I might believe it is some kind of ore tied to a mythical beast called the [Luxum beast](Luxum%20beast). A rare being and while they think only 1 exist. Both the [Vrael Olo](Vrael%20Olo) and [Kecaulas](Kecaulas) fight over these deposits. 

We than went on my command to the [Vrael olo](Vrael%20olo) direction, shortly before passing the territorial border they approached, believing me to be one of them. They were purebloods, beings closer to the graceful [Lamia](Lamia) than I ever could. However they are cursed with indoctrination and while showing the same elegance akin to our people. They still held to savage believes of superior and inferior beings based on race rather than skill. Calling me a traitor before attacking. They were no match, the male lieutenant’s head is added to [Leona](Leona)’s kill count, her snakes now lay with me while I sleep. During our hunt she remained our captive but I found out that taking away the blatant idiology that she is a great ‘galpal,’ so I had her locked up as a diplomat.

  

We ended our mission with a non aggression pact with the [Kecaulus](Kecaulus) and they helped us capture several large prey that we escorted towards Haven.
