Where there used to be nothing but slums, a haphazard arena was put together as these slums were completely annihilated by the [Nirant Flow](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Haven.%20the%20shifted%20city%20(credits%20luca)/Nirant%20Flow.md) as were many other parts of the city. When there aren’t any bounties or missions to be completed [Striders](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Striders.md) come here to have sanctioned bouts with each other. Some of the guards come here as well. The river was forced to go underground around here from the landing of the city on top of it. On the provided map the river seems to keep going, but keep in mind that around this district the river goes underground.
