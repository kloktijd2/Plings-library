 Strider Report – Mail, Rats, and Sneaky Thieves
Party: [Haru](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Haru.md) (me), [Cain](Cain), [Orchid](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Orchid.md), [Ber](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Ber.md)
We met up at the post office with [Cain](Cain), [Orchid](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Orchid.md), and [Ber](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Ber.md), who is a funny turtle
man. The secretary sent us to the avi avo aviry… pigeon room. [Orchid](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Orchid.md)’s cat
immediately started chasing the pigeons, and a halfling man was running
around trying to stop it. He told us one pigeon was missing, so we had to help with
post office work around town.
Our job was to deliver outgoing mail, collect incoming mail, and keep people safe
while doing errands. I was given a very important letter that must not be bent.
We went house to house delivering mail. At one house, a dog was guarding the door.
I tried to sneak past it, but it saw me and bit me. I wasn’t mad it was just doing
it’s job. I gave it some food and pets after, and it seemed okay with me.
At another house, an old lady had a [rat](rat) problem. We found some [rat](rat)s in a closet
and caught them quickly. But then we noticed more in the ceiling. [Cain](Cain) did
something a bit extreme and brought the whole ceiling down, and a huge swarm of
[rat](rat)s fell out. I slipped on one I had already stepped on earlier, but my team
handled the rest with spells and we cleared them all out.
We continued and saw a man carrying two packages. I remembered the packages
because I drew a cute flower on one of them. He tried to run when we talked to him,
but [Ber](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Ber.md) and I chased him across the rooftops. For a big turtle, he is really fast. I
would like to race him someday.
I think [Ber](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Ber.md) got too close during the chase and the thief fell off the roof. [Cain](Cain) told
us to redeliver the packages while he handled the thief. When we came back,
there was ash, burned grass, and part of a building looked slightly charred. I’m not
sure what happened there.
Later we checked the news and met the sheep-heard again. [Betsy](Betsy) the sheep is still
healthy, but there is a new problem where something is scaring the sheep at night.
We went to investigate and found a ghost. After a bit of a fight and [Cain](Cain) using
holy water the ghost disappeared. I hope it is okay wherever it went.
We finally delivered the “do not bend” letter, and it turned out to be a family
portrait. After that, the sun started to set and the streets got darker.
We helped a boy who lost his cat. [Ber](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Ber.md) climbed a tree, but the cat got scared and
jumped down. I caught it before it got hurt. It was just too afraid to come down on
its own.
On our way back to the post office, we noticed three suspicious people listening to
us. I had an idea, so we talked loudly about a very expensive painting we had
delivered earlier and pretended to leave it unguarded. The people took the bait and
tried to break into the house. We stopped them and arrested the thieves before they
could steal anything.
Job well done. Thieves caught. Painting still safe. I think that was a good day
