Report on the Material Gnome Mission by [Khan Bloodhand](Khan.md) 27 jun 11([pling note](pling%20note.md): unsure 2024)

  

We arrived at the great [Tall Spire](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Points%20of%20intrest/Tall%20Spire.md) with a tiny manling. This tiny Manling seemed to be our boss. 

He called himself [Renwyl](Renwyl) of [Caelycion](Caelycion) a banker with no experience in the wild, not smart to go out.

  

We went to go to the [Tall tower](Tall%20Spire.md) via the mage portal in [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md). 

It had 10 doors, 

According to the small armoured dwarf they took door 6 last time filled with water.

[Khan](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Khan.md) does not like water and the banker decides to go to the 5th one. 

  

The door led us to a magical world cloaked in an illusion of a large grass field with a long path going to a stone unremarkable house.

This world does not follow the rules of magic and runes and instead works on its own laws. Created by the entity inside this plain. 

  Khan

We found a small water basin in front of the door. In it was an oily substance, [Khan](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Khan.md) took this but it disappeared when arriving back into our world. Inside this oil was the hilt of a sword, the sword however seemed to be as illusionary as the world itself. For it had a blade none could see and none could touch.  
  

When entering the house we could see a dining hall. Windows that were not visible on the house exterior now lightened the room alongside a lot of candles and torches. The weather seem to darken from bright blue to a darker hue. 

  

We found in the hall 3 painting, one of a corridor full of doors and windows letting in a storm wind at the end of the corridor stands a figure holding a purple flame. A second one of a room full of mirrors with once more a figure with a purple flame in the reflection. The last painting showed a cistern with in the middle a statue once more holding the same purple flame.

In the hall we also located the exit at the fireplace, it was covered by magic bars who could not be opened by lass [Maeve](Maeve.md) her magic or brute force. With a purple candelabra in front. 

  

With every second the darkness outside worsened and enemies seemed to appear from outside. Spectral hands and faces phased through the windows and were focused on darkening the room. The wall, now fully in the dark, became a living being intent on devouring us. Making it clear that we should avoid the darkness at all costs. 

  

Due to the idiotic genius of my brother jumping into the cistern painting, we figured out that our task is to go into the paintings and get the purple candles located in the paintings. Upon entering the paintings they changed form. A drowning man for the cistern, a skeleton pointing at a black hole for the corridors, a man looking into his own skeleton for the mirrors. 

  

It was a horrified sight, the house itself turning to us and the darkness taking both me and the tiny banker surrounded by it. It took him much harder than myself and I believe the little one is never going outside again. Should you ever go back into the house. Then take a lantern each, for the [ghost](ghost) can take an hour of fuel per attack.

  

Eventually, we got all of the candles and managed to get out through the fireplace. Returning to the tower and being greeted by a chest of loot and the pay promised by the tiny man.
