## The Garrison

Not everyone was quite adventurous enough to go out of the city to venture the dangerous [Fallow Lands](Fallow%20Lands). Some of those people manned the patrol around [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md) and put together a small garrison where they could train to protect the city in case of a straight attack. There were multiple ex-soldiers who came together to lead this effort. Over time one of them took the lead and became known as [The Comés](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Haven.%20the%20shifted%20city%20(credits%20luca)/The%20Comés.md).
