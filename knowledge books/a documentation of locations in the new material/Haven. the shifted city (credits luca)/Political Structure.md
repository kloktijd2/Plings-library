[[Haven]] is governed by a group. This group consists of exiled nobles from [Halycion](Haven.md) and other influential people. Each single district has a person that oversees it. These people gather every 2 weeks to speak on important matters concerning their districts and greater Haven. This group is known as the Haven Cabinet. The cabinet has by amount of ‘districts that people live in and aren’t controlled by a different group of people’ 10 members. Beyond these 10, other districts are governed by: The [Head strider](Audry%20Hound.md) ([[Guild District]]), [[Pholenor]] / [[Ovastrea]] / [[Zoviar]] ([[Magician’s Ward]]), The [[Reeve]] ([[Civic District]]), [The Comés](The%20Comés.md) ([Garrison](The%20garrison.md)). The ten ‘regular’ cabinet members run on terms of 2 years an=d are appointed by the permanent members being the other six. The identity of the assigned ten is kept a secret.
