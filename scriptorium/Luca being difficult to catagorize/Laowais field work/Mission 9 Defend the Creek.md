Finally we would be moving on with my plan to conquer and stabilise the entire creek. The conquering part we had done before. See report of [Deep Dive](Mission%206%20Deep%20Dive.md) for more information. Now it is the task to fully settle and claim [the creek](Resin%20Creek.md) and the resin sea as our own. Who knows how far the shores reach. It took a lot of convincing at the council and to convince the head strider to send a semi-decent druid alongside us to speed up the construction of the fort. I do not understand why they did not merely send some more workers but I should be satisfied with what the guild has given. 

We had earlier cleared out the lake and the construction of the fort began at the shores of the [Sahuagin Lake](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Sahuagin%20Lake.md), previously known as [Beaver lake](Beaver%20lake), 2 days North-West from Haven. When we arrived we quickly moved to the first step of our plan. We were quick to scout the immediate surroundings and create a tent camp with a wooden palisade around us. We were provided with a number of [guild](Striders.md) soldiers including the Blackbridge Highwaymen who I took under my personal wing. The scouts found nothing and I found nothing in the water, a crab even assured me nothing has been moving. Quite disturbingly, seeing I had expected an early [Sahuagin](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Sahuagin.md) assault, yet they would not come this mission, seemingly licking their wounds from our last assault. 

During the evening of day one we had a discussion with [the giant Elk](the%20Great%20Elk.md), the spirit of Resin Creek. It did not seem to hold us in great value apart from [Yvalon](Yvalon). We explained our purpose and I ensured it knew that this was all according to the [guild](Striders.md)s wishes and as an ally it should move out of the way, the fort was made on the opposite bank of the creek nonetheless. 

During day 2 we moved onto the next phase. Many workers started cutting down trees for both their resources and opening the area. The druid seemed to have some issues here and there but a quick reminder that the [Guild](Striders%20Guild) will benefit from this outpost was enough to calm her down for the mission. The soldiers and scouts patrolled the area while [Yvalon](Yvalon), [Rudy](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Rudy.md), and the wizards assisted the druid in work. Around noon we were attacked by a small collection of [soultorn](soultorn) [undead](undead). They were not capable of disturbing our works and were swiftly taken care off but their attack was rather irregular. Instead of attacking when we approached their position like usually. These seemed to attack our position without provocation. 

At the end of day 2 we got a visit from the most annoying set of [pixies](pixies) and [fairies](fairies) I have yet to forget. They were too optimistic, energetic, and loud to my liking, almost like children. Luckily [Yvalon](Yvalon) the darling was able to keep them occupied and they even provided our stone tower with an arcane cannon it seems they have their use. Alongside this orange [ooze](ooze) was spotted in the river and [Henka](Henka) managed to capture part of it.  

On day 3 we started our day with a strange appearance. [Ghouls](Ghouls), grey in colour and odd in behavior. They stared at us from the other side of the river. [Henka](Henka) gave chase but we could not figure out more details about them because they retreated into the thick forest. We continued our plan, a second palisade wall was build, watchpost and towers were set up, and even more trees were cut down. Our scouts found another group of [skeletons](skeletons). We tracked them down to a cave where they seemed to be under the orders of a [soultorne](soultorne) [undead](undead) captain, a [necromancer](necromancer) of sorts with a large blade. We escaped and at night they attacked us. They put up a strong fight but we won with minimal casualties, partially due to the cannon on the tower. The first defence of a mighty fortress, its attacker falling into dust. 

The next few days went on rather uneventful. We increased the size of the perimeter, added another set of fortifications and secured the waterline, still fearing the attack of the fish. As another precaution I ordered that the remaining wild wood scattering the area around the fort be doused in oil as a good defence. This defence came clutch as during a strong storm an attack came from the river, but it weren't the [Sahuagin](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Sahuagin.md). [Animated trees](Animated%20trees), [mephits](mephits), and other walking shrubbery attacked us. The rain covered their natural weakness to fire but due to the oil they were quick to burn nonetheless. We took casualties but not all too many problems. The second attack on the fort has been thrown off and we can finally solidify our position as the masters of this River. I will take this fort as justification to crown myself River Princess of Shu.
