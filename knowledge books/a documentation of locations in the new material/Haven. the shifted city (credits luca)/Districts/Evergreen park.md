Evergreen Park used to be an enormous forest at the border of Halycion. Only a small part got taken with and as of today nearly all trees inside Haven have been cut down for reconstruction, the park holds the only remaining trees of [The Prime Material](knowledge%20books/is%20it%20a%20bird.%20No%20its%20a%20plane/The%20Prime%20Material.md) except for the one in the temple of [Calaryn](knowledge%20books/world%20religions/Gods/Calaryn.md). It has been tended to by the followers of [Calaryn](knowledge%20books/world%20religions/Gods/Calaryn.md). Only because of them are there any Prime trees left to begin with.
