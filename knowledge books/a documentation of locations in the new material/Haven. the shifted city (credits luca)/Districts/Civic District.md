The civic center was just a simple building when it was on the outskirts of [Halycion](Haven.md). Now it is where all the business of the town comes together. As of this, there is a lot of quickly cobbled together construction here from after [The Shift](knowledge%20books/Modern%20history%20and%20events/The%20Shift.md) as Haven was in dire need of effective governing. The district was plopped down around the [Nirant Flow](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Haven.%20the%20shifted%20city%20(credits%20luca)/Nirant%20Flow.md), portions had to be rebuild to acclimate to the river.

# Townhall

The center of legislation in [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md), the town hall is where The Reeve does her business. As seen before, the [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md) Cabinet gathers here every 2 weeks.

- Often used for meetings between [Ealdormen](Ealdormen) that convene to discuss laws and recent issues. Such as criminal activities, civil disputes, legal treatises…
    
- Cleaned daily to maintain an organized ‘look’.
    
- A place of noise and quiet, business and calm, discussion and agreement.
    
- Gathering place for taxmen.
    
- Administrative and governmental function.
    
- Cultural activities, such as art shows, stage performances, exhibits, and festivals.
