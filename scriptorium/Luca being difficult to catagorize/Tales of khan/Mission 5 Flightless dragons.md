Report on the Flying Stones expedition written by [Khan Bloodhand](Khan.md) 12 dec 2024

(Transcribed by Guild Clerck [Laowai Xhê](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Laowai%20Xhê.md)) 

  

We moved out a day or 2 after the [Dragon](knowledge%20books/animals%20of%20the%20new%20material/Creature%20types/Dragon.md) attacked [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md). Normally Lady [Frill](ZZ%20idk%20yet/Frill.md) would lead this expedition to satisfy her curiosity however she seemed to have her hands full dealing with the wounded from that find. Me, [Barrek](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Barrek.md), and [Vivian](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Vivian.md) would move out ourselves and report back to her. 

  

We left through the North Western gate, and we followed the northern bank of the [Fish people](Fishfolk) River, (I believe he might mean the [Sahuagin](knowledge%20books/factions%20near%20haven/Sahuagin.md)) believing this was where we could find the Aviary. After half a day of travelling, we decided to set up camp near the edge of [Resin Creek](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Resin%20Creek.md) Forest, giving [Vivian](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Vivian.md) time to make her tree move with magic. We were interrupted by some weird beast troop led by some big deer-looking thing. It seems against the magic of [Vivian](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Vivian.md) but after [Barrek](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Barrek.md) started talking to squirrels it seems just concerned where we dispose of it. Making sure we have to keep it in the forest and she was allowed to continue the ritual. 

  

During that night we got a visit by a [Strider](templates/Strider.md) with many magic items. She called herself [Shanty](Shanty) and appeared to bring letters for [Vivian](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Vivian.md) and my Brother. These were congratulations for killing something called a [hag](ZZ%20idk%20yet/hag.md) or something. We seemed suspicious for other reasons but [Barrek](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Barrek.md) decided to read her mind to see if she really was a strider. This took too long so I went to sleep, I wasn’t woken to the sound of battle so I suppose they must have been alright. 

  

After continuing our travels we arrived at the usual spots. The [FIsh People](Fishfolk.md) portal with the two towers and a chain, and a day after that the [Beaver Lake](Beaver%20Lake). On the way here there seemed to be some hostilities as a spear was lobbed at us from the water. Still need to convince [Frill](ZZ%20idk%20yet/Frill.md) to have us find these [river trolls](river%20trolls) and aide them in this fight against the fishes. Moving North from [Beaver Lake](Beaver%20Lake) we found many trees marked by the Fish. After a bit we once again moved into the [Everplains](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Everplains.md) dotted with smaller ruins. Camping here we found 2 trolls that seemed friendly, they were also enemies of the [hag](ZZ%20idk%20yet/hag.md) and seemed to hang out with a bloke named [Ulridan](Ulridan). We continued West, [Vivian](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Vivian.md) stepping on one of the thorns she said is affiliated to the [hag](ZZ%20idk%20yet/hag.md), Maybe this would be the same ones that Dear [Leona](Leona) told me about she saw around an effigy. This time a vile lightning storm started to rise for the rest of the expedition. However, those templefolk who say that lightning is a way for god to smite the unjust seem to be wrong. Or their god must be really weak. I took 2 bolts directly and barely made a scratch.

  

Once more we eventually reach a familiar-looking [Resin Creek](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Resin%20Creek.md) Forest after half a day, even seeing the river again as my map might suggested. However, moving further West for a day we found a different forest with enormous trees that made me feel surprisingly not big. We continued until we found some very large [dragons](dragons). Most of these did not fly or breath fire, there seemed to be one that walked on 2 legs with a very large jaw and one that seemed to be the largest beast the Bear must have made rivalling its size. [Vivian](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Vivian.md) told me these were not dragons but I am not sure if I am sure about that. They seem to fit the bill. The ones with the big jaw were called [Tyrano](Tyrano) ([Khan](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Khan.md) seems to stutter with the name), A [trex](trex). However out of nowhere came something resembling more of a monster version of this [Trex](Trex) to attack the two, swallowing one whole. We joined the fight and [Vivian](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Vivian.md) allowed me to become such a fierceless [trex](trex) with [Barrek](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Barrek.md) riding my head into battle. Safe to say we killed it and took its flesh, bones, teeth, and leather. 

  

Eventually, we decided that [the Aviary](the%20Aviary) was not gonna be found, it seems we did not guess right on what way we should have taken. Next time perhaps try going directly west from [Haven](knowledge%20books/a%20documentation%20of%20locations%20in%20the%20new%20material/Areas%20and%20cities/Haven.md). We decided to finish our quest going north once more. Apparently, if we went west any further we would have reached an unending collection of water. I dislike that idea. (The sea?) After a day of travel, we reached a hilly terrain and some time even later we arrived at something I haven’t had the pleasure of seeing before in this world. A castle made from blackened stone. It seemed strong and well-placed, reminding me a lot of those castles the [humans](humans) were in when we came for missions. 

  

Both my brother and [Vivian](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Vivian.md) seemed somewhat afraid of it, I told them the only reason that they should be afraid is the ballistas on top but if they are not manned what is the harm? [Barrek](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Barrek.md) heard a voice in his head, a lady called[ Osanna Von Sclaer](%20Osanna%20Von%20Sclaer) seemed to invite us for dinner, promising us a feast we wouldn’t forget. I got interested but both him and [Vivian](profiles%20of%20people/strider%20profiles/Vivian.md) declined and seemed to go away. This pissed off our host who send some tiny things out for us. It was a tough fight, they used a lot of magic. But we eventually killed them without to much issue. Sadly they faded when we did, there was no head to bring back home. Seems to be a dangerous place for newer people, don’t go without protection.
