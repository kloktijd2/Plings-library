**Revenge of the Demons**

[[Ber]], [[Widdle]], [[Ado]], [[Orchid]]

We leave from [[the Verge]] as the sun rises, we meet each other and head one day NW.

An hour into our travel, we meet an old man that we trade some food with for a rabbit. He started insulting us and said that the [[Wicked Copse]] brings out the best in people. [[Jan]] is annoying.

After 4 hours, we reach the [[Wicked Copse]], it has an impenetrable treeline. It has an aura that brings about some primal. There is no real wildlife in the forest. There are bodies hanging from nooses in the forest.

We enter the forest and see a red glow in the distance. There is a primal fear trying to enter our mind, but we resist. The forest has been infected by a [[demonic]] infection, and that it has been happening for a while.

We hear a thing flying towards us above the treeline, we cannot see it. We hide and see that it is a bone-white medium creature made of spines. We think it was hunting. We continue. ([marked for investigation](marked%20for%20investigation.md))

In the distance we hear humanoid screams. We run to investigate and see 4 people hanging by their necks with their heads screaming. We see 4 people hanging and screaming. We free one, and its face grows bat wings and attacks us. We barely survive and take a healing rest after the fight.

During the healing rest, [[Ber]] is visibly cursed. We investigate him and find that if we don’t remove his curse, he turns into a head-bat thing. We rush back to [[The Verge]] to help him. The [[Strider]] that helps us tells us of a temple in the East in the [[Wicked Copse]] that seems worth investigating.

We come across some ruins and see that it is more arcane inclined than holy. We hear footsteps and hide, but the footsteps disappear. We enter the temple and see a big hole that is 100ft. deep.

We head down the hole. It is a dark hall with piles of bones and pillars. The hallway is very smooth and the bones are of many varieties, many species and of different times.

We investigate one of the edges, and see a statue of a mage in a wall, with bloody writing on it. It has written in common “give in to greed, and hoard the world’s gold”. [[Ado]] steals one gold coin from [[Widdle]] and waves it in front of the statue, it does nothing. We see roots approaching from the right side of the statue. There has been a demonic influence over the entire place.

We follow the roots and see rotten target practice dummies. We come across another statue covered with roots with blood writing “give into wrath, and soak your blade in red”. The statue is also of a mage, but a different mage.

We follow the roots and find a door with 4 mages engraved, across the door is “those unpure of soul can enter this domain”. Two of the mages on the door match the statues that we already found.

We hear footsteps in the distance.

We find another mage statue “give into pain and scream until your throat stops”. [[Orchid]] sends Dot to investigate the footsteps, and it gets shot.

We investigate the last statue with “give into despair and drown in your tears”.

Ado speaks the quote aloud and starts crying, magic start to hold but he fights it off. [[Widdle]] speaks quote 3 and starts screaming, so hard that he takes damage.

[[Orchid]] speaks quote 2 and starts raging towards [[Widdle]]. We attack her and free her from the curse, but she feels it lingering.

Ber speaks quote 1, and has the compulsion to steal everyone’s gold. He takes [[Widdle]]’s and [[Ado]]’s gold, [[Orchid]] turns her gold into wood. He refuses to give it back.

A moment later, the curse takes hold again. [[Ber]] gives [[Widdle]] and [[Ado]] holy water, releasing the curse from them.

We sneak and find 5 [[skeletons]], one of them has a dark aura around them. We surround them and attack.

We destroy the one with the aura, but the [[spirit]] remains, it dives into a pile of corpses and jumps out as a skeleton again.

After a long fight, we destroy the [[spirit]]. When we leave the hole the curse disappears. We leave.

We sense the [[demonic]] influence behind the door, but that can only be handled by a more powerful party. ([marked for investigation](marked%20for%20investigation.md))
